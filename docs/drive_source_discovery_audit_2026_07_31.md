# Authenticated Drive Source Discovery Audit — 2026-07-31

## Purpose

This audit closes the authenticated Google Drive discovery pass that followed the 46-paper local-corpus re-mining campaign. It records what was searched, what was newly admitted, what was already subsumed, what required deeper notes, and what was deliberately excluded. Discovery is not evidence promotion: connector-readable private text can guide the manuscript while remaining unpublished and source-reported.

## Search scope

The pass searched authenticated Drive for `Portia Synapse`, `TreeLLM`, `Spider Synapse`, `AI architecture`, `whitepaper`, `white paper`, `technical specification`, and `research paper`, then followed architecture-family and title links surfaced by those searches. Candidate documents were compared against `sources/source_inventory.json`, local raw caches, existing source notes, and pinned project-mining dossiers.

## Deliberate non-admission

- `Stargate` was inspected at the discovery level and excluded because it concerns speculative plasma/wormhole physics rather than AI-stack architecture, evaluation, governance, implementation, or resource systems. Its presence in Drive is not a reason to stretch the book's scope.
- Packaging, product, business-plan, and generic task-system appendices repeated inside composite files were not admitted as separate technical sources unless they contributed a unique book-relevant mechanism.

## Evidence boundary

Authenticated connector access establishes that the text was available for review. It does not publish the private text, verify source-reported code or results, establish priority, supply independent corroboration, or promote a book claim. Same-author variants and embedded copies count once. New mechanisms are integrated with explicit non-claims or converted into research obligations when implementation or causal evidence is missing.

## Re-run trigger

Repeat this discovery audit when a new paper title is added to the project root or Drive, when a canonical source changes materially, or before a major book release. The next pass should compare content identity and section families, not just filenames.
