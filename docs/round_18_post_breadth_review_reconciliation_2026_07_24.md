## Findings with teeth

### Apparatus-to-evidence imbalance — accepted

The repository's schemas, validators, formal models, receipts, and review
machinery are valuable only when they improve the reliability of a result. They
are not a substitute numerator. Future progress reporting must lead with
claim-bearing natural outcomes, competence failures, or exact blockers—not
word, source, validator, proof, or artifact counts.

### P2 is stable under the completed breadth transaction — accepted

P2 tests governed admission on natural repository work. Perception, embodiment,
mixed organizations, multi-agent dynamics, and inner alignment add important
owners, but they do not invalidate P2's fixed governance construct. The
moving-target argument no longer justifies postponing P2.

### One competent result changes the project's evidentiary category — accepted

The next categorical objective is not a favorable result. It is one
competence-qualified natural, non-authored, independently evaluated result with
complete denominators and lifecycle cost. A negative or inconclusive outcome
counts when the implementation, construct, evaluator, sensitivity, fair-rescue,
and custody gates pass. A broken setup or weak positive control does not.

### Breadth should close on an explicit boundary — accepted and already executed

Round 18 admitted five distinct-owner chapters, integrated seven section-scale
gaps into existing owners, and restored the structural freeze. The remaining
seven research candidates are not an active chapter queue. A future admission
requires a dated roadmap amendment and a completed material empirical or
evidence checkpoint.

### “Zero empirical results” — narrowed

The repository contains bounded synthetic, prototype, local-observation, and
internally evaluated empirical records, including conservative and negative
dispositions. What it does not yet contain is the first
competence-qualified natural, non-authored transition, external reproduction,
SOTA-supported result, or chapter-core promotion. The roadmap targets that
exact missing class.
