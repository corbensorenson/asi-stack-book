## Decision

The review correctly identifies three remaining manuscript risks: the
twenty-owner depth queue needs faster concept-complete execution; atom presence
does not guarantee claim-addressable decomposition; and the depth phase needs
a clean handoff into later proof and evidence work. Its word-count concern also
exposes a real implementation contradiction: the Round 20 prose called 5,000
words diagnostic, while the validator rejected a contracted chapter below that
number.

That contradiction is removed. A repaired chapter now completes only through
its named concept contracts plus a chapter-specific semantic disposition bound
to the exact QMD SHA-256. Crossing 5,000 words is neither necessary nor
sufficient. Regeneration cannot renew a semantic review after prose changes.
The first three reviews are preserved at their exact Round 20 digests and
remain editorial receipts with no evidence effect.

### 2. Claim-addressable atom adequacy

The current unified custody result remains 84/84, but nineteen chapters have
five or fewer atom references and nine have one. Those counts are diagnostics,
not proof of debt. For every chapter entering a concept tranche, audit each
material claim-bearing concept against its atom projection.

An atom is adequate only when it records a stable owner and identity,
proposition, scope, falsifier, promotion ceiling, evidence route, contrary
evidence or failure boundary, non-claims, and consumer handoff. Each material
concept needs one such bounded atom, or an explicit many-to-one justification
showing why a shared atom preserves independent falsification and promotion.
Split only where claims can fail or mature independently. Never split to reach
a count target.

### 3. Projection-aware de-duplication

Keep W3 terminal for the reader-facing projection. Add a separate raw-scaffold
ownership audit before closing P6.9. For every widest-spread raw block, record:

- exact normalized text or fingerprint and chapter spread;
- whether it is reader-visible, generated, structured, or source-only;
- authoritative owner and regeneration path;
- semantic purpose and whether identity itself requires repetition;
- disposition: `centralize`, `generate`, `rewrite_domain_specific`, or
  `retain_with_reason`; and
- a negative control showing that copied reader-facing prose still rejects.

The exit condition is zero unjustified widest-spread raw blocks, not zero raw
repetition. Necessary generated reconciliation and typed tables may remain
when centralized or generated ownership is clearer and safer than bespoke
copies.

### 4. Clean evidence handoff

Depth work does not promote evidence. The later proof/evidence phase may treat
a chapter as ready only after its concepts are semantically reviewed at an
exact digest and its material claims are atom-addressable. The handoff packet
must preserve the chapter, concept, atom, source, falsifier, evidence lane,
maximum inference, and unresolved challenge identities.

P6.9 does not close merely because all chapters cross 5,000 words. It closes
when the twenty queued owners have terminal concept dispositions, all accepted
repairs have current digest-bound reviews, every material concept is
atom-addressable or has a justified many-to-one mapping, the raw-scaffold
ownership audit has no unjustified block, W3 remains terminal, and the exact
proof/evidence handoff is written. No empirical, formal, safety, release, SOTA,
AGI, or ASI conclusion follows.
