## Decision

The review identifies a real manuscript-depth problem, but several of its
specific coverage and inheritance findings describe an older repository state.
The corrective program therefore keeps the useful diagnosis, rejects stale
denominators, and makes depth recovery a prose-first, concept-by-concept
transaction.

The chapter count is frozen at 84. No new chapter may enter merely because a
topic keyword is absent, a word count is low, or an idea sounds adjacent to the
thesis. Existing owners must be improved first. The freeze remains until the
current thin-chapter queue is empty or every remaining short chapter has a
dated, chapter-specific reference-role justification that passes manual
semantic review.

## Findings that do not survive current-main inspection

- Dangerous Capability Domains already has an explicit CBRN program and
  biological/chemical threat-model treatment.
- Content Authenticity already has explicit deepfake treatment and a bounded
  interface to the current EU Article 50 transparency guidance.
- Societal Resilience already addresses fraud and scams, child safety,
  non-consensual intimate imagery, incident reporting, cyber misuse,
  biological and chemical misuse, manipulation, and mental-health and
  parasocial harms.
- The current W3 inheritance packet reports reader-facing/editorial repeated
  12-grams falling from 812 to 0 and the broader raw-QMD diagnostic falling
  from 1,921 to 936. The claim that boilerplate rose by 17 percent is not a
  valid description of the current authoritative packet.
- The reported twenty atomless chapters came from reading the frozen base
  registry without its separately versioned addenda. Unified coverage was
  80/84 before this transaction. The only uncovered current owners were
  Military AI, Confidential and Verifiable AI Computation, Human–AI
  Symbiosis, and Relational Dimension Compilation.

These corrections do not imply that the chapters were deep enough. Keyword
presence is not concept substance, and atom presence is not evidence.

## Findings accepted

The complete tracked-QMD diagnostic found 23 of 84 chapters below 5,000 raw
words, with a median of 6,982. Word count is a triage signal, not an
acceptance test, but the cluster is too large to ignore. Existing validators
also checked broad section shape without requiring each important concept to
state its own mechanism, failure mode, non-claim, and source-specific
grounding.

The first repair tranche deepened:

1. `dangerous-capability-domains-and-misuse-uplift`,
2. `content-authenticity-watermarking-and-synthetic-media-integrity`, and
3. `societal-resilience-and-misuse-defense`.

All three now exceed the 5,000-word diagnostic trigger. Their 24 named priority
concepts pass the mechanical substance contract: a named section of at least
150 words with separate mechanism, failure-mode, non-claim, and declared-source
grounding. Manual review remains mandatory because a label and a word count
cannot certify useful explanation.

The append-only Round 20 atom packet adds five bounded responsibility atoms for
each of the four genuinely uncovered chapters. Unified current-manifest atom
coverage is now 84/84. The packet does not rewrite the historical base
registry or earlier addenda and does not move support.
