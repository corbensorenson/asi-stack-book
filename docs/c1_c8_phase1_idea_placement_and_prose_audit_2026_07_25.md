# C1–C8 Phase 1 Idea-Placement and Prose Audit

Recorded: 2026-07-25

## Decision

The ASI Stack is in a prose-first convergence phase. The immediate objective is
not to increase theorem counts or open protected experiment outcomes. It is to
make every accepted architectural idea explicit, assign it to a canonical
owner, connect it to the human reading route, and preserve the evidence ceiling
that applies today.

No new chapter is required for C1–C8. The existing 84 chapters already contain
the necessary responsibility boundaries. The missing work was synthesis:
several cross-cutting ideas were precise in the roadmap but absent or implicit
in the manuscript. This transaction moves those ideas into their owners and
installs a 22-unit reader route over the complete 84-chapter reference.

## Phase boundary

Phase 1 may improve definitions, explanations, interfaces, diagrams, handoffs,
glossary entries, ownership maps, research questions, comparator sets, and
falsification conditions. It may organize existing proof and experiment work by
the claim it could eventually support. It may not treat organization as proof,
promote a support state, report an unexecuted outcome, or let a synthetic
fixture decide a broad natural claim.

Phase 2 begins only after the all-chapter gate below passes. It then rationalizes
the existing formal estate, removes vacuous or duplicate proof rhetoric, binds
the smallest conclusions to consumers, and runs competent natural evidence for
the final bounded conclusions. Proof volume is not itself an objective.

## Proof and experiment restraint

The C3 calculus, C4 developmental loop, C5 trusted kernel, C6 depth overlay, and
C7 flagship design are architecture at `argument` support. Their appearance in
the book does not imply a new theorem, implemented service, competent
instrument, positive result, refutation, reproduction, transfer, safety,
readiness, SOTA, AGI, or ASI.

When Phase 2 opens, it should prove or test only conclusions a named consumer
needs. A useful P0 or P1 result may remain narrow. A negative natural result is
admissible only after construct, implementation, baseline, evaluator,
sensitivity, rescue, denominator, and cost gates establish that the idea
received a competent attempt. Inconclusive is a valid terminal outcome.
