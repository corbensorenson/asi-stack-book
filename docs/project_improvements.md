## Recommendation in one paragraph

Make the existing project easier to trust, run, read and falsify before expanding it. Restore publication admission and portable validation; reject malformed protocol records; distinguish frozen evidence from current projections; make the 26-unit reader the clear narrative entry; simplify duplicated maintenance; connect the most important proof assumptions to real consumers; and concentrate empirical effort on the three defended contributions through the already governed claim-state and natural-work experiments. The goal is a smaller burden of maintenance supporting stronger observations, rather than a larger inventory of artifacts, validators or architectural names.

## How to use this document

The recommendations use stable IDs such as `A01` and `E04`. They are a decision catalog, not 114 simultaneous tasks or a promotion quota.

- **P1:** restore a broken intended workflow or a core validation boundary before the next publication that depends on it.
- **P2:** improve research discrimination, runtime fidelity, maintainability or reader correctness.
- **P3:** useful editorial, operational or presentation improvement after its prerequisite is ready.
- **Confirmed:** supported by inspected code, a reproduced failure or a recorded diagnostic.
- **Evidence gap:** a stronger conclusion lacks an observed result; this is not automatically a software defect.
- **Strengthen:** an existing mechanism is useful but can be made more reliable or easier to consume.
- **Conditional:** investigate or implement only when the named need occurs.

Each item explains a change and a completion condition. The owner references name existing files or workstreams; they do not appoint a new authority. Completion means the stated behavior is verified and its remaining limits are explicit. It does not mean the associated architecture claim has been promoted.

The complete repair list and research suggestions are intentionally broader than the current work-in-progress limit. Keep that limit when selecting execution work. Merge related items into one coherent change, and retire a recommendation when an existing mechanism already satisfies its completion condition.

**P2 · Strengthen.** Cover numeric bounds and types, arrays and uniqueness, references, required fields, extra fields, patterns, enums and optional formats. ### C03. Generate event sequences, not only isolated mutated records

**P2 · Strengthen.** Use stateful/property-based testing for grant, dispatch, revoke, retry, crash, observe and compensate sequences. **Done when:** at least one important runtime lifecycle is checked against an independently specified model, failures shrink into replayable sequences, and fixed regression examples are retained. Generated authored tests remain mechanism evidence, not natural-workload performance evidence.

### C04. Keep independent consumers independent of the producer

**P2 · Evidence gap/strengthen.** An independently named script is insufficient if it imports the same acceptance function or reuses the same mistaken state inventory. For load-bearing consumers, document shared libraries, assumptions, input transformations and evaluator dependencies. Share mundane parsing deliberately while separating the semantic oracle where feasible.

**Done when:** a deliberately broken producer can be rejected by the consumer, the tested independence is named precisely, and shared failure modes remain visible. Do not multiply scripts merely to manufacture the appearance of independence.

### C05. Report a valid baseline before evaluating rejecting mutations

**P2 · Confirmed diagnostic defect.** The P6.9 handoff validator subtracts baseline error strings from mutation errors. A stale baseline and a further corrupted digest produce the same message, leading to “negative mutation accepted” even though both records are rejected. Separate invalid-base reporting from mutation-sensitivity testing.

**Done when:** an invalid baseline is reported once and cannot yield misleading mutation-acceptance claims; a valid baseline is accepted; each mutation's acceptance outcome is checked directly. Preserve the substantive stale-digest failure.

### C06. Add an aggregate diagnostic mode without weakening gates

**P2 · Strengthen.** The runner stops at the first failed unit, making a large maintenance audit expensive to iterate. **P2 · Strengthen.** For each chapter, state the decision or failure class it owns, its inputs, outputs, boundary and nearest overlapping owner. Use the outline's chapter jobs and existing substance contract. Resolve confusing overlaps in prose before proposing a structural merge.

**Done when:** readers can explain why adjacent chapters are separate and where a new source or bug belongs. A source influx does not automatically create a chapter; a genuine new responsibility can still justify one under the established admission contract.

### J02. Put the concrete problem before the record vocabulary

**P2 · Strengthen.** Start an owner with a specific failure and explain the invariant it violates. Then introduce the record that makes the failure addressable. Move comprehensive field inventories behind a readable mechanism explanation or into reference subsections.

**Done when:** a reader understands why the contract exists before encountering its long schema, and every field has a role in the mechanism or a clearly named future obligation. Concision must preserve the evidence boundary.

### J03. Separate conceptual explanation from implementation inventory

**P2 · Strengthen.** Resource Economics, Integrated Reference Architecture and Artifact Graphs are especially large technical owners. Give them a short conceptual path, then clearly labeled deeper implementation/evidence subsections. Preserve stable anchors and public custody. The reader edition can summarize rather than reproduce the entire technical inventory.

**Done when:** someone can follow the chapter's argument without reading every fixture, historical reconciliation and implementation detail, while a researcher can still reach those exact records. This is not blanket deletion of useful reference material.

### J04. Consolidate recurring noninheritance explanations

**P2 · Strengthen.** Authority, evidence, implementation and legitimacy repeatedly fail to inherit from one another. Keep concise local caveats where meaning requires them, but teach the common rule once and cross-reference it. Spend additional prose on the chapter-specific counterexample and mechanism.

**Done when:** removing repeated boilerplate does not remove the only caveat near an important claim, and adjacent chapters sound distinct rather than like replacements of nouns in the same template. Use semantic review in addition to repeated-phrase detection.

### J05. Stabilize terminology and distinguish overloaded names

**P2 · Strengthen.** Reconcile contract, lease, receipt, qualification, assurance, authority, observation, evidence, residual, rollback and compensation across owners. Name whether a layer is a logical responsibility, runtime service, artifact interface or institutional role. Keep specialized author terms tied to ordinary concepts.

**Done when:** the same term does not silently switch meaning between chapters, and the glossary directs readers to the canonical owner. A distinctive name is retained where it identifies a substantive mechanism rather than creates novelty by vocabulary.

### J06. Use testable chapter summaries and handoffs

**P3 · Strengthen.** End with what was argued or established, which assumptions remain and what the next owner must preserve. Replace self-referential “this chapter explained” prose with the actual conclusion. Link a decisive test or current artifact where appropriate.

**Done when:** a reader can state the owner contract and evidence ceiling from the summary, and the handoff names the actual adjacent responsibility without promising unimplemented behavior. Update adjacency through the manifest workflow.

### J07. Keep minimum implementation and mature target clearly different

**P2 · Strengthen.** The smallest honest artifact may be a finite guard, a local service or a qualified experimental instrument. The mature target may require independent institutions, external effects and transfer. Say exactly which obligations bridge those stages and which have been fulfilled.

**Done when:** the minimum is runnable or has a concrete blocked dependency, and the mature target cannot be mistaken for a current result. Avoid vague aspirational feature lists and mechanically inserting a disclaimer without clarifying the missing mechanism.

### J08. Consolidate structure only through semantic and URL custody

**P3 · Conditional.** The reader already compresses the 89 technical owners into 26 narrative units. Further technical merges should occur only when responsibilities and invariants genuinely duplicate, or navigation cannot explain their difference. Preserve source assignments, claim identities, proof/test hooks, redirects and history through the existing consolidation process.

**Done when:** a merge reduces reader confusion or maintenance cost without losing a distinct boundary or breaking public lineage. Do not set a target chapter count merely to make the project look smaller.

**P2 · Strengthen.** Add 320-pixel and enlarged-text checks to representative current reader and technical pages, including long tables, figures, edition controls and headings. ## Completion measures and things to avoid

### Measures that would demonstrate progress

- A fresh declared environment can execute each required CI tier and reports unavailable capabilities truthfully.
- Every remaining current-validator failure has a corrected owner or an explicit governed disposition; no required gate is merely skipped.
- Frozen evidence remains verifiable as history while current projections change without false historical failure.
- One canonical input edit has a known, reviewed set of dependent current updates.
- The publication candidate, tested bundle and remotely observed site identify the same eligible revision.
- Invalid schema cases, broken joins and meaningful fault sequences reject at their intended boundary.
- Load-bearing proof assumptions have visible runtime/trust mappings and transitive proof dependencies.
- A natural eligible task reaches a preregistered discriminating terminal result with full cost, failure and selection accounting.
- Readers can find the current narrative, explain a boundary and reach its exact technical/evidence owner.
- Maintenance duration and intervention decrease without losing required behavior or claim discipline.

### Measures that would not demonstrate progress by themselves

More chapter files, sources, theorem declarations, passed schema fixtures, validator scripts, generated reports, rendered videos, target-length units, artifact digests or green dashboards. Each can be useful, but each needs a meaningful consumer and appropriate evidence scope.

Avoid automatically promoting all chapter cores because their components pass; requiring every idea to become a flagship; describing synthesized author material as independent corroboration; proving record completeness as if it established real-world truth; counting a self-report as an independent observation; deleting failed attempts; endlessly renegotiating competence after outcomes; replacing historical hashes with current ones; adding filler to pass word thresholds; and adding approval steps the active policy does not require.

No language/framework migration, new universal orchestration platform, wholesale chapter rewrite, bulk archive deletion or full media campaign is justified solely by this review. Prefer the smallest change that repairs a real boundary or improves a discriminating test. A competent simpler alternative winning is a useful outcome.
