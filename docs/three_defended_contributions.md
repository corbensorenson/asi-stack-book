# Three Defended Contributions

Last updated: 2026-10-11

The book remains broad, but the research program now permits exactly three
program-level defended contributions. The older five-track portfolio remains
useful as subordinate work packages; it is no longer permission to present five
independent flagship claims, much less one flagship claim per chapter.

Here, “defended” names the three **proposed targets selected for scrutiny**;
it does not report established novelty or a successful general defence. The
current proposals are an architectural composition, a public evidence process,
and a composition of observation and residual accounting. Their bounded local
implementation results retain their own evidence ceilings. The
[prior-art comparison](defended_contribution_prior_art_positioning.md) specifies
a competent conventional workflow for each target and the relation it must
add. Equal behavior at lower burden favors that alternative and narrows the
contribution claim.

## Contribution type and present result

A proposed composition and a demonstrated implementation are different objects.
The first describes a useful relation to investigate; the second reports what
an exact artifact did under its stated assumptions. Neither establishes a new
mechanism merely because its interface has a coined name.

The comparator notes retain different intake depths. A note based on metadata
is positioning, not a fresh full-paper review; selected passages do not import
the source's reported efficacy. The [ordinary challenger blueprints](defended_contribution_prior_art_positioning.md)
can carry all the proposed relations in their own vocabulary. The composition
must earn usefulness across its declared handoffs after full burden is counted.
No new mechanism has been established at the program level. A future claim of
one would need an exact mechanism, relevant antecedents, a discriminating
comparison and evidence at that scope; publication of an author manuscript
supplies lineage without making that decision.

The [signature-idea typing](contribution_novelty_ledger.md#contribution-type-interpretation)
places the nine supporting ideas within these composition/process proposals.
Existing `distinctive_mechanism_ablation` fields name the behavior removed in a
bounded comparison. “Distinctive” there distinguishes experimental conditions;
it does not assert originality. Those frozen ladders keep their exact wording,
controls, results and support ceilings.

## Governed-cognition interface contracts

The defended target is the join between responsibility, authority, context,
planning, verification, execution, replacement, and review: typed contracts
should preserve refusal, narrowing, rollback, and accountable handoff state.
The governed repository-change slice and authority/revocation trace invariants
are the shared executable lane. This is not a claim of complete ASI, deployed
enforcement, alignment, safety, or optimal modularity.

Primary owners are System Boundaries and Authority, Intent-to-Execution
Contracts, Recursive Self-Improvement Boundaries, and Integrated Reference
Architecture. The other assigned chapters supply mechanisms or integration;
they are not separate flagship contributions.

## Public claim-state transition discipline

The defended target is a living technical work that makes claim identity,
support movement, negative evidence, review, scope, and prohibited inference
explicit enough to block silent promotion and evidence laundering. The shared
lane is the claim/evidence ledger, transition gates, contradiction controls,
and adversarial counterexample pressure testing. This does not prove the decision process is
epistemically optimal, that reviewers are correct, or that any chapter thesis
is true.

Primary owners are Evidence States and Claim Discipline, Claim Ledgers and
Belief Revision, Executable Specifications and the Lean Proof Envelope, and
Living Book Methodology.

## Record/reality reconciliation and residual honesty

The defended target is that receipts and ledgers become useful governance only
when independent observation can challenge them and unresolved effects, costs,
failures, and rollback gaps remain conserved as owned residuals. The shared
lane is the matched governed/baseline workload, receipt mismatch, hidden
residual, rollback, and governance-overhead measurement. This does not prove
complete observation, tamper-proof logging, safe governance, efficient
oversight, or production reliability.

Primary owners are Artifact Graphs, Compact Generative Systems and Residual
Honesty, and Resource Economics and Token Budgets.

## Chapter assignment rule

`products/contribution_focus_contract.json` assigns all 89 manifest chapters
to exactly one primary program contribution. Eleven are primary owners; the
other 78 are supporting or integration chapters. Every assignment explicitly
sets `independent_flagship_claim` to false. The contract is generated in
manifest order and checked by `scripts/validate_contribution_focus_contract.py`.

The map is a scope-control mechanism, not novelty proof, evidence, external
review, or support-state movement. A primary owner can still fail its
falsification target, be narrowed by prior art, or remain at `argument`.

## Exit ladders

The generated contract now gives each contribution one bounded route out of
argument-only discussion without creating a promotion quota. Every ladder
names the claim slice, closest implemented comparator, simplest baseline,
positive and instrument controls, mechanism ablation, strongest alternative
explanation, success/negative/inconclusive dispositions, support ceiling, and
next consumer.

These ladders describe bounded implementation checks and mechanism ablations.
They do not establish superiority over competent conventional architectures.
The contract's `prospective_ordinary_comparison` references the unexecuted
challenger blueprint for each target. Broader comparative claims require their
own eligible evaluation; the existing routes and frozen protocol keep their
recorded identities.

For governed-cognition contracts, the current comparator is the P5-U1 record-only route
and the simplest baseline is direct execution with the
ordinary source-link check. The behaviors isolated by the full route are
admission, recovery, and compensation; the replay does not establish their
novelty. Because the successful repair was known
before replay, the current result remains bounded local implementation evidence
and cannot estimate natural-task utility.

For claim-state discipline, the simplest baseline is **JSON Schema validation**
without exact claim-identity, competence, and maximum-inference checks. The
ablation asks whether shape-valid records can still launder support. Passing
repository controls cannot prove evidence truth, evaluator competence, or an
optimal epistemology.

For record/reality and residual honesty, the bounded target joins receipts to
**independently read Git state**, retained effect history, compensation, and
residual counts. The ablation keeps an executor report while removing terminal
state reads and residual conservation. Local Git transparency and shared
repository ownership remain strong alternative explanations, so opaque-service
and independent-observer transfer stays open.

The machine-readable ladders in `products/contribution_focus_contract.json`
are authoritative. A positive outcome may move only the exact bounded non-core
mechanism named by its accepted evidence transition. A negative outcome narrows
or rejects that mechanism. Instrument failure produces no substantive result.
