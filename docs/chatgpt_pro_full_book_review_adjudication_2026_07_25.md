## Decision rule

This review is useful editorial and research-program pressure, not external
evidence for any chapter claim. Each criticism is accepted only when it matches
the current repository, identifies a plausible causal defect, and can be
translated into a bounded owner, artifact, test, and completion gate. Praise,
grades, proposed terminology, and architectural suggestions do not move claim
support.

Historical snapshots remain valid for their exact commits. They cannot define
the current book, and a stale review observation cannot be copied into the
roadmap as current truth.

## Executive disposition

The review correctly identifies the project’s present bottleneck:
**integration and natural evidence now matter more than additional breadth**.
The response is not to delete the 84-module architecture or defer worthwhile
ideas. It is to separate the complete architecture reference from a much
smaller narrative interface, concentrate empirical work on one governed
repository-change flagship, and replace repeated chapter-local contract prose
with a shared formal and operational kernel.

The controlling architectural law is **noninheritance**:

- capability does not grant authority;
- context does not become belief or permission;
- a plan does not authorize an effect;
- a receipt does not prove reality;
- a theorem does not prove its model or runtime binding;
- a successful candidate does not inherit qualification;
- and an improvement proposal cannot ratify itself.

The stack is the structural consequence of that law.

## Defended core

The existing three-contribution contract remains the right organizing surface:

1. **Governed-cognition interface contracts** — the authority-to-effect and
   noninheritance chain.
2. **Public claim-state transition discipline** — explicit, asymmetric,
   non-laundering evidence movement.
3. **Record/reality reconciliation and residual honesty** — independent
   observation, effect reconciliation, and conserved residuals.

No fourth flagship contribution is added. Routing, developmental intelligence,
trusted-kernel minimization, bounded liveness, and the transition calculus are
necessary mechanisms or evaluation dimensions for those three claims.
