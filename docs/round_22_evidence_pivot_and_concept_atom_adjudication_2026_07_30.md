# Round 22 evidence pivot and concept-atom adjudication

Scope: evidence-phase priority, reader projection, concept-linked claim

Support effect: none

## Decision

The review's main direction has teeth: the breadth and concept-depth phases are
closed enough that the roadmap must now optimize for competent empirical
attempts and evidence dispositions, not additional surface area. P2-R3a is the
headline execution lane. Its next legal action remains exact: restore at least
the frozen 50 GiB host-capacity floor, restore Docker-daemon access, and rerun
the immutable preflight before materialization. The documented failure is a
blocked attempt, not a negative result about governed repository admission.

The review also identifies a real falsifiability weakness. Nineteen chapters
had five or fewer atom references, and nine routed eight distinct reviewed
concepts through one composite core identity. Atom count remains a diagnostic,
not a target. However, concepts that can fail, narrow, or mature independently
need independent custody.

Round 22 therefore adds 104 concept-linked atoms for the thirteen flagged
chapters that already have eight current, digest-bound, semantically reviewed
concept contracts. Each atom preserves a stable owner, proposition, scope,
falsifier, source boundary, evidence lanes, promotion ceiling, and non-claims.
The chapter-substance contract now maps each of those concepts to its own atom.
This reduces the low-atom diagnostic from nineteen chapters to six and the
single-atom diagnostic from nine to zero without moving support.

## Findings already satisfied

The review's corrected reader-surface recommendation is valid but already
implemented. `reader_release` removes `Chapter status`, `Codex test plan`,
claim labels, evidence metadata, source crosswalks, drafting guardrails, and
other live/research scaffolds while `research_release` preserves them. The
current projection check removes 403 live-only sections across 84 chapters.
This is edition governance, not a new prose de-duplication campaign.

Visual freshness is also already enforced. Every visual packet binds the
canonical chapter-content digest, excluding only the managed embed block. A
chapter edit makes the current visual stale, removes the current embed, retains
the predecessor as stale custody, and requires a new generation before
publication. The visual validator covers all 84 chapters and rejects freshness,
generation, predecessor, embed, publication, and custody mutations.

## Accepted work

### Evidence pivot

- Freeze new chapter and taxonomy growth unless a distinct-ownership defect is
  demonstrated; novelty alone is insufficient.
- Keep P2-R3a as the first empirical headline. Do not lower the capacity floor,
  bypass the Docker predicate, erase the failed attempt, or infer mechanism
  failure from infrastructure non-materialization.
- Once the preflight passes, execute the frozen run, preserve all logs and
  residuals, and disposition only the exact claims the run reaches.
- Let a negative or no-change result count only after implementation
  competence, positive controls, strong baselines, evaluator sensitivity,
  budget sufficiency, and the rescue ladder have passed.

### Remaining concept-linked decomposition

The following six low-atom chapters do not yet have named eight-concept
contracts and must not receive mechanically generated atom parity:

1. `human-factors-and-meaningful-control-in-oversight`
2. `privacy-data-rights-and-information-flow-governance`
3. `governed-world-models-and-reality-grounding`
4. `governed-model-training-distributed-optimization-and-scaling`
5. `white-box-evidence-interpretability-and-activation-governance`
6. `governed-operations-incident-command-and-graceful-degradation`

For each owner, conduct a whole-chapter semantic audit first. Identify only
material concepts that can fail or mature independently. Each retained concept
must name its mechanism, strongest counterclaim, falsifier, source contribution
and limitation, required evidence, promotion ceiling, adjacent-owner handoff,
and non-claim. A chapter may finish with fewer or more than eight concepts.
Completion is zero unowned material concepts, not a chosen atom count.

## Boundaries

The Round 22 addendum is editorial and evidence-routing infrastructure. It does
not prove any concept, change a core-claim support state, create an accepted
evidence transition, authorize a release, or establish safety, performance,
SOTA, AGI, or ASI. The original composite atoms remain immutable historical
custody; the concept-linked atoms are additive current identities.
