# ASI Stack reader exercises

Try the first sitting and one other passage. Allow up to fifteen minutes for
the first sitting. You can stop or skip a question. Before showing the
result or discussing the passage, the facilitator asks for your first
decision and which line, fact, or record mattered. Then say what would
change your mind and where you first looked. The facilitator may rotate
which second passage readers receive.

## First sitting

Open the [first sitting](https://corbensorenson.github.io/asi-stack-book/reader/quickstart.html#first-sitting).
Make the approval decision before opening the replay. Afterwards, explain
what changed on disk and what the passing test established. If the book
made you hunt for the outcome or accidentally showed the answer, say where.

## A second passage

1. [The failed upgrade](https://corbensorenson.github.io/asi-stack-book/reader/chapters/unit-01-asi-is-a-stack-not-a-model.html#the-change-that-worked).
   The rollback command succeeds and the failing client still gets an error.
   What can the maintainer report to the service owner?
2. [The appeal](https://corbensorenson.github.io/asi-stack-book/reader/chapters/unit-07-constitutions-moral-uncertainty-and-objective-formation.html#service-eligibility-appeal).
   The intake office corrects the record. The dispatch partner has yet to
   acknowledge it. What can the reviewer close? Who should seek the next
   observation?
3. [Where should the lesson live?](https://corbensorenson.github.io/asi-stack-book/reader/chapters/unit-21-adjudicated-persistence-generalization-feedback-continual-learning-and-unlearning.html#decision-exercise-where-should-the-lesson-live)
   Make the decision in the pickup-code exercise. Then say what would let
   the local team retire a dated reminder.
4. [The cache the rollback missed](https://corbensorenson.github.io/asi-stack-book/reader/chapters/unit-22-evaluation-readiness-thresholds-and-structured-assurance.html#decision-exercise-cache-migration).
   Make the rollout decision. What would change your ruling for a canary
   outside the affected scope?
5. [One verifier, three speeds of change](https://corbensorenson.github.io/asi-stack-book/reader/chapters/unit-25-recursive-improvement-replication-and-containment.html#three-speeds-of-verifier-change).
   The queue empties after the verifier starts approving its successor.
   Which measured improvement should the team rely on? What should it
   inspect next?

Before the participant reads the unit's closing synthesis, ask them to
write their first decision and cite what changed it. Then offer the
corresponding transfer prompt and collect the answer before discussion.

- **Unit 9.** A substitution improves internal summary quality. Its grant
  covers internal summaries. A separately tested customer-notification
  path remains approved for the previous implementation. Which work may
  the new implementation take today? Which path can serve customers?
- **Unit 15.** Three calendars show a service was delivered on Monday.
  All three imported one booking event. The claim is that the client
  attended. What can you support, and what one observation would you seek?
- **Unit 21.** A spreadsheet routinely exports the wrong delimiter, and
  this week's destination separately requires an expiring account label.
  Where should each correction live? Which cases must pass before you
  adopt a permanent safeguard?

After the response, score the choice, the cited evidence or authority,
and the condition that would reopen the choice separately. For Unit 9,
look for distinct grants and a still-qualified existing route. For Unit 15,
look for the shared booking source and evidence of attendance. For Unit 21,
look for a reproducible export check, a dated account record, and a test
that includes a valid export. Equivalent justified answers earn credit.

## Facilitator record

With permission, retain an anonymized note containing the revision or
preview identity, prompt, elapsed time to first decision, first place the
reader looked, decision, evidence named, proposed next observation, and
any navigation or wording that got in the way. Record any guidance you gave
before the answer. Keep optional comments separate from the initial
response. Publish only anonymized feedback the participant has agreed can
be shared.

Use the [review notes](../reader_manuscript_review.md#reader-trial-for-the-revised-decision-paths)
after the response. For the first sitting, a reader should distinguish
successful configuration checks from permission to change both files.
Look for equivalent defensible answers, then use recurring issues to guide
a revision and rerun the affected prompt. The facilitator records any
change against the text that the participant actually saw.
