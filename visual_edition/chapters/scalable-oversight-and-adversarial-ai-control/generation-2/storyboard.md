# Generation-2 storyboard — Scalable Oversight and Adversarial AI Control

## Experience target

The video is a worked incident, not a protocol inventory. A green replacement
candidate conceals a high-impact cohort; a critic surfaces it; a separately
routed auditor verifies two unsafe outcomes; and the candidate moves to
quarantine through four owners that never collapse into one another. The second
half generalizes that trace into consumer binding, material-change re-admission,
calibrated abstention, a non-aggregating operating scorecard, and the exact
argument-level evidence ceiling.

Signature visual: the green aggregate stays green while the red cohort opens
and the action route turns toward quarantine. This contradiction should be the
image a viewer remembers.

## Art and motion rules

- The green aggregate must remain visible during the red cohort reversal; do
  not recolor it red, because the lesson is that the score can survive while
  the decision fails.
- The auditor never emerges from the debate transcript. Its route starts on a
  separate lower path and enters a frozen cohort replay.
- Firewalls are literal lines, not decorative separators. Every owner remains
  on its own side after motion settles.
- Correlation is encoded by shared dependency edges, never by a warning label
  alone.
- Quarantine retains the candidate and attached record; nothing disappears.
- Quiet intervals must preserve an inspectable diagram. Avoid idle blank cards,
  continuous camera motion, spins, shakes, and decorative particle effects.

## Audio and accessibility

Narration is the only locked audio layer. Mix target is approximately −16 LUFS
integrated, no true peak above −1.0 dBTP, and no unexplained silence of three
seconds or more. Color is redundant with role labels, enclosure, position,
line style, and crosses. All routes settle into named outcomes. Captions occupy
the safe lower zone without covering active labels, and the descriptive
transcript names every material reveal, dependency, block, quarantine,
invalidation, and boundary.

## Evidence ceiling

The candidate, roles, outcome replay, and operating values are synthetic. The
video explains an argument-supported design rationale. It does not establish
reviewer competence, independence, calibrated protocol value, safety,
deployment readiness, transfer, AGI, or ASI performance, and it changes no book
claim or authorization state.
