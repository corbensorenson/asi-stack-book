Safe posture does not always mean silence. The system may preserve defenses or seek a fresh observation. The important point is that the response is chosen prospectively, not improvised by the model under pressure.

Off-ramps belong in the design before the crisis. Authority can be staged. Geographic scope can be narrow. A reversible mode can precede an irreversible one.

The same discipline applies to decommissioning. Removing a model is incomplete if credentials and dependencies still allow its route to act.
