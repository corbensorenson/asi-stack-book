A synthetic warning exercise begins with two sensor tiles and twelve minutes to decide. The AI reports high confidence. Then provenance opens: both tiles copied one upstream feed. The clock compresses to two minutes. Corroboration was one claim repeated twice.

That is why military AI is not one autonomy score. Decision support, defensive automation, targeting support, and autonomous force application occupy different roles, authorities, affected populations, reversibility, and escalation sensitivity.

A component can classify its test data correctly and still destabilize the larger interaction. Strategic stability is a joint claim about sensors, people, command chains, communications, adversaries, time, and possible off-ramps—not a property stored inside one model.

Start with a mission envelope: purpose, environment, affected population, legal-review state, command chain, time horizon, and termination conditions. A vague objective cannot authorize a precise effect.

Then bind authority and effect. Separate permission to observe, recommend, approve, and act. Constrain target class, prohibited effects, scope, abstention, communication-loss behavior, and human confirmation.

Meaningful human judgment is not an icon beside a button. The accountable person needs time, context, alternatives, competence, independence from automation pressure, and power to reject, delay, narrow, or stop.

Draw a deliberation budget. Verification, consultation, legal review, and communication are not empty latency. Removing them changes the decision architecture even when the final human click remains.

Now reopen the warning trace. Every observation carries source, time, transformation, uncertainty, integrity, and correlation receipts. Two displays derived from one feed count as one lineage, not two independent witnesses.

Ambiguous, spoofed, stale, or correlated observations must remain typed as such. A model may help compare hypotheses, but fluency cannot turn missing provenance into truth or uncertainty into authorization.

Abstention needs a route. Sensor conflict, integrity failure, communication loss, and out-of-envelope conditions resolve to a declared safe posture with an owner, duration, monitoring rule, and re-entry gate.

Safe posture does not always mean doing nothing; inaction can preserve a hazard. Compare bounded alternatives without letting urgency manufacture authority or erase legal constraints.

Watch advice approach the command boundary. Repetition, ranking, countdowns, and defaults can make a recommendation function like an order. Reveal uncertainty, alternatives, provenance, and who owns the decision.

Timeline compression is itself an intervention. As seconds disappear, verification paths close and diplomatic off-ramps vanish. A faster local loop may create a more brittle strategic system.

Now add a second actor. Each side observes the other's deployment, updates expectations, changes posture, and shortens its own clock. Safety cannot be inferred by evaluating one side while holding the counterpart still.

Reciprocal deployment can produce proliferation, entanglement, and common-mode failure. Shared models, datasets, suppliers, or doctrines may correlate mistakes that a one-system test treats as independent.

Use an interaction matrix: false alarm, spoof, ambiguous attribution, communication loss, disagreement, adversary adaptation, reciprocal automation, and escalation. Preserve decision time, reversibility, human burden, and residuals.

Prospective off-ramps belong inside the design. Rate limits, reversible modes, narrow geography, staged authority, positive confirmation, independent monitoring, suspension, and decommission must exist before the system creates pressure to continue.

Secrecy complicates assurance but does not erase it. A restricted annex carries sensitive traces under access and retention controls. A public card states method, uncertainty, governance, incidents, and what redaction prevents others from checking.

Independent reviewers need enough access to challenge the mission, authority, evidence, interaction model, and safe posture. A reviewer who sees only the developer's conclusion is not an independent evaluation channel.

Incidents stay linked to model, mission, operator, observation lineage, authority, effect, and response. Suspension freezes effect. Decommission revokes credentials, archives receipts, removes dependencies, and preserves residual ownership.

The failures are visible: automation bias turns advice into command; compressed time removes deliberation; correlated sensors create false confidence; tactical optimization externalizes civilian harm, escalation, or long-run instability.

Today this chapter provides a design rationale, a command-and-interaction taxonomy, and a public-safe governance architecture for synthetic scenarios. It does not test a deployed military system, authorize a weapon, establish lawful use, or measure strategic stability.

Its maximum inference is narrow. Mission, authority, provenance, judgment, safe-posture, interaction, off-ramp, secrecy, and decommission records make responsibility inspectable. Complete records do not prove truth or effectiveness.

Military AI becomes governable when local model performance remains subordinate to accountable command, interaction-aware evaluation, preserved decision time, bounded effects, and reversible authority.
