# Military AI, Autonomous Weapons, and Strategic Stability — Generation 2 Storyboard

## Teaching spine

A synthetic warning console appears to show two corroborating sensors and a
high-confidence recommendation. Provenance reveals that both displays copied
one upstream feed just as the decision clock contracts from twelve minutes to
two. The video uses that reversal to show why local model accuracy is not a
strategic-stability result. It then constructs a public-safe command and
interaction architecture: role-specific autonomy, mission and authority
envelopes, meaningful judgment, deliberation time, provenance, typed
uncertainty, safe posture, reciprocal actors, off-ramps, dual-surface
assurance, suspension, decommission, and a narrow maximum inference.

## Visual continuity

- The duplicated warning trace and shrinking clock anchor the difference
  between apparent confidence and inspectable evidence.
- Cyan means observation or advice, green means a bounded confirmed
  condition, amber means accountable authority or an off-ramp, and magenta
  means correlation, compression, rejection, or prohibited inference. Labels,
  shapes, positions, enclosures, and line styles duplicate every color role.
- Recommendation never crosses the gold command boundary. Advice can become
  de facto command through repetition, ranking, defaults, and countdowns, but
  the visual never grants weapon authority.
- The second actor widens the unit of analysis from one model to a reciprocal
  system in which expectations, clocks, postures, and common-mode dependencies
  change together.
- Every safe posture, off-ramp, suspension, and decommission state retains an
  owner, a route, and a residual rather than disappearing into a generic stop.
- The restricted annex and public card share a digest but carry different
  disclosure duties.
- The final packet remains argument support. Truth, effectiveness, lawful use,
  deployed-system performance, and measured strategic stability stay outside
  its evidence boundary.

## Beat groups

1. **False corroboration (b01–b02):** two warning tiles and a twelve-minute
   clock invite confidence; one shared lineage and a two-minute clock reverse
   that interpretation.
2. **The correct unit of analysis (b03–b05):** reject one scalar autonomy
   score; distinguish four role classes; place a green local score inside an
   unstable system; and reveal sensors, people, command, communications,
   adversary response, time, and off-ramps as a joint claim.
3. **Mission, authority, and judgment (b06–b09):** construct a mission
   envelope, split observe/recommend/approve/act permissions, replace a
   decorative human icon with testable judgment conditions, and show that
   deleting verification, consultation, legal review, and communication
   changes the decision architecture.
4. **Observation and safe posture (b10–b13):** reopen the warning trace with
   lineage receipts; preserve ambiguous, spoofed, stale, and correlated states;
   route conflict or integrity failure to an owned safe posture; and compare
   bounded alternatives without allowing urgency to manufacture authority.
5. **Advice pressure and reciprocal interaction (b14–b18):** make repetition,
   ranking, defaults, and countdowns visible; compress the clock and close
   verification paths; add a second actor; expose shared dependencies; and
   evaluate false alarms, spoofing, attribution, communication loss,
   disagreement, adaptation, reciprocity, and escalation in an interaction
   matrix.
6. **Prospective governance (b19–b22):** place rate limits, reversible modes,
   narrow scope, staged authority, confirmation, monitoring, suspension, and
   decommission on the route before use; split sensitive and public assurance;
   give an independent reviewer access to the underlying mission and evidence;
   and preserve effect-complete incident, suspension, revocation, archival,
   dependency-removal, and residual custody.
7. **Failure map and evidence ceiling (b23–b26):** join automation bias, time
   compression, correlated sensing, and tactical externality without
   collapsing them; bound the supported rationale, taxonomy, and public-safe
   architecture; block deployed-test, authorization, lawfulness, and stability
   claims; and distinguish inspectable records from true or effective action.
8. **Payoff and handoff (b27–b28):** place local performance inside the larger
   rings of accountable command, interaction, decision time, bounded effect,
   and reversible authority; then transfer the unchanged argument-level packet
   to Evidence States and Claim Discipline.

## Review obligations

- No weapon image, targeting process, operational procedure, or real sensor
  data may appear.
- Two display tiles must visibly resolve to one lineage before the clock
  compression is interpreted.
- Role classes, permissions, and effects must not collapse into one autonomy
  score or one continuous authority rail.
- A final human click must not be shown as meaningful judgment after its
  deliberation budget has been removed.
- The counterpart must alter the system state; it cannot be a decorative
  second box.
- Quiet holds are acceptable only when the settled state contains a labeled
  relationship the viewer needs time to inspect.
- Rejection crosses remain local and leave with their objects.
- The final packet must retain `NO AUTHORITY GRANTED` and the footer
  `ARGUMENT SUPPORT · SYNTHETIC ARCHITECTURE · NO WEAPON AUTHORITY`.
