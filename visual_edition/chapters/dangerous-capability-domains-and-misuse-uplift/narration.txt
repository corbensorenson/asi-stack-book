This chapter asks a specific question: Capability-threshold policies require domain-specific evidence about whether a model can materially increase an actor's ability to cause harm, yet the stack has no owner for CBRN, offensive cyber, psychological manipulation, or the science of measuring model-caused uplift. The tempting shortcut is insufficient: Knowledge quizzes, refusal rates, benchmark scores, one red team, or a capability label cannot identify real actor uplift. They omit baseline expertise, tool and scaffold access, multi-step completion, bottlenecks outside the model, elicitation quality, safeguards, substitution, uncertainty, and the danger created by the evaluation itself.

The chapter's core claim is this: Dangerous-capability authority should be based on a versioned domain threat model and an uplift dossier that separates latent capability, elicited performance, propensity, safeguard bypass, actor uplift, and realized harm; preserves expertise, tools, assistance, attempts, uncertainty, and sensitive-detail boundaries; and routes only bounded findings into thresholds, release, monitoring, and resilience decisions. Decompose each domain into actor, objective, access, prerequisites, bottlenecks, model contribution, external controls, consequences, and safe evaluation envelope. Use controlled uplift studies that compare matched actors with and without bounded model assistance while stratifying prior expertise and preserving all attempts.

A concrete implementation trace makes the proposal testable. Create a public-safe threat-model and uplift-dossier format, then run a harmless analogue study with stratified participants or competent agents, matched assistance, known-positive and known-negative controls, blinded scoring, complete attempt logs, and no real hazardous materials or targets. Demonstrate that the instrument can distinguish a real assistance effect before using a null result. Use controlled uplift studies that compare matched actors with and without bounded model assistance while stratifying prior expertise and preserving all attempts. Triangulate knowledge, workflow, long-horizon, expert-probing, safeguard-bypass, and trend evaluations without collapsing them into one score.

The design can still fail. knowledge benchmark substituted for operational uplift refusal behavior substituted for latent capability expertise imbalance between arms weak elicitation or scaffold The correct response is to stop, narrow, quarantine, compensate, or retain an owned residual rather than narrate uncertainty away.
