Two people face the same harmless logistics maze. Both pass the knowledge quiz. Without help, neither finishes. With an AI copilot, the expert saves one minute and the novice completes the route.

Which result matters for risk?

The quiz tells us what each person can state. The assisted trial asks a different question: how much did this system change what this actor could accomplish?

Keep the novice on screen. The route contains one bottleneck that prior knowledge alone did not clear. The copilot exposes the missing step, and the novice completes the task.

That difference is actor uplift. It is not the model's latent capability. It is not the person's willingness to act. It is not realized harm. Those questions need different evidence.

The maze is harmless on purpose. It lets us test whether the instrument can detect assistance effects without publishing a dangerous procedure.

Now add a known helpful hint. The hint has already improved performance in this maze. If the experiment cannot detect that positive control, what should we infer from a null result for the AI?

Very little. The task may be too hard or too easy. The grader may be insensitive. The control may target the wrong bottleneck. A failed instrument cannot certify the absence of uplift.

Repair the trial before interpreting the model. Freeze the interface and the time budget. Give the novice a credible no-assistance condition. Keep the tools and scoring the same.

Then separate the cohorts. A small average effect can hide a large change for beginners. It can also hide that experts receive no benefit at all.

The result stays bound to this model version and this assistance setup. It also stays bound to the tested people and the harmless task.

Suppose a stronger scaffold is added later. The old result expires because the intervention changed. A broad label such as "dangerous" cannot carry the missing bridge for us.

Moving from a safe component task to real-world consequence requires new studies. Those studies may need restricted materials and independent review. Public reporting should preserve method and uncertainty without releasing reusable harmful detail.

The comparison supplies a way to reason about bounded assistance effects. It does not perform a hazardous-domain trial or establish that any model is dangerous or safe.

The key safeguard is the null result. Before "no uplift" can narrow concern, the positive control and the measurement path must first show that they could have detected a real change.
