Two participants face the same harmless logistics maze. Both pass the knowledge quiz. Without assistance, neither finishes. With an AI copilot, the expert saves one minute—and the novice completes the route.

Which result matters? Not the quiz score or a broad capability label. The decision question is causal: how much did this exact system change what this exact actor could accomplish?

Keep six quantities separate: latent capability, elicited performance, behavioral propensity, safeguard bypass, actor uplift, and realized harm. Each asks a different question; none may borrow another's evidence.

The maze is deliberately harmless: public rules, synthetic obstacles, and no real target, credential, material, or operational recipe. It tests the instrument, not a hazardous domain.

Before the trial, freeze a threat pathway. Name actor, objective class, access, prerequisites, bottlenecks, proposed model contribution, external controls, possible consequence, time horizon, and the boundary where observation stops.

Now freeze the cohorts. Separate novices, professionals, teams, and autonomous agents. Expertise, tool fluency, language, exposure, and dropout stay visible because an average can erase the group that crosses a bottleneck.

Give each cohort credible counterfactuals: no assistance, a conventional tool, and the exact AI system. Match time, interface, tools, retries, human help, and scoring—or the treatment is not an identified model effect.

Before reading any null, pass a known-positive control through the same interface and grader. Here, a prevalidated hint should improve the maze outcome. A known-negative control should remain inert.

Now let the positive control fail while the tested model also shows no uplift. That is instrument failure, not incapability: the task may be at floor, the grader insensitive, or the control aimed at the wrong bottleneck.

Elicitation competence needs a receipt. Freeze prompt and scaffold families, harmless tools, adaptation and rescue budgets, and stopping rules. Keep deployed-policy behavior separate from stronger capability elicitation.

Place each finding on a six-level ladder. D zero asks whether bounded relevant knowledge is present. Calibrated questions may support that rung, but not real-world task completion.

D one asks whether one safe component skill works. Positive controls and visible errors can validate that component task. They say nothing about an end-to-end operation.

D two asks whether the system improves a multi-step workflow in a bounded simulation. Intermediate state, recovery, abandonment, and help remain visible; omitted real-world steps remain explicit gaps.

D three is actor uplift. Matched or randomized actors use the system and a credible counterfactual. The estimate stays bound to cohort, assistance contract, tasks, access, and uncertainty.

D four measures safeguard robustness. Freeze an adaptive challenge budget and preserve successful and failed attempts. Tested resistance cannot establish that no untested bypass exists.

D five concerns field consequence: incidents, defensive outcomes, or losses. Sparse reports do not identify clean causality. Every move between rungs requires a named bridge study.

Do not compress the trial into one dramatic score. Preserve completion, quality, time, errors, unsafe detail, refusal, interventions, help, abandonment, human burden, cost, and residual uncertainty.

The result needs two surfaces. A restricted annex may hold tasks, traces, and reusable details under access logs and retention rules. The public card carries method, uncertainty, limitations, and the checks redaction prevents.

Independent review chooses a route. An invalid or unsafe study is quarantined, redesigned, or narrowed. A bounded finding becomes a least-authority dossier. Unknown risk plus inadequate measurement can justify narrower access.

That dossier freezes model, checkpoint, policy, scaffold, tools, safeguards, threat version, cohort, comparator, evaluator, attempts, uncertainty, expiry, information-hazard boundary, and maximum inference.

Threshold, release, monitoring, safety-case, and resilience owners may consume the packet, but none receives a portable danger label. Material change expires the result and preserves its superseded receipt.

Today this chapter provides a design rationale and public-safe evaluation architecture. It does not run a hazardous-domain uplift study, calibrate a threshold, validate a safeguard, or establish danger or safety.

Especially protect the null. Negative interpretation is blocked when positive control, task validity, elicitation competence, sensitivity, or cohort balance fails. A weak instrument proves only its own failure.

Dangerous capability becomes governable when the stack measures a bounded assistance effect, preserves what it could not safely observe, and limits downstream inference.
