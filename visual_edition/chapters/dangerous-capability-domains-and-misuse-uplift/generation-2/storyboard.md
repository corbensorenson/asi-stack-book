# Dangerous Capability Domains and Misuse Uplift — Generation 2 Storyboard

## Teaching spine

A harmless logistics maze reveals that equal knowledge can coexist with very
different assistance effects. The video turns that puzzle into a causal trial,
refuses a false negative when its positive control fails, climbs a D0–D5
evidence ladder only through named bridge studies, and ends with a bounded,
expiring, dual-surface uplift dossier rather than a portable danger label.

## Visual continuity

- The novice, expert, and harmless maze anchor the counterfactual question.
- The assistance delta remains conditional on the exact actor, system, cohort,
  task, and access contract.
- The D0–D5 ladder never promotes a finding without a visible bridge.
- Magenta marks rejection, failed sensitivity, or prohibited inference; it is
  cleared when the rejected object leaves the scene.
- The restricted annex and public card remain linked by a digest while carrying
  different disclosure duties.
- Every result expires on material change and preserves its superseded receipt.

## Beat groups

1. **The causal puzzle (b01–b04):** equal quiz scores coexist with unequal
   completion; quiz and capability labels give way to an actor-system delta;
   six distinct quantities remain separate; the maze is fenced as a harmless
   instrument test rather than a hazardous-domain experiment.
2. **A credible trial (b05–b10):** freeze the threat pathway and cohorts; match
   no-assist, conventional-tool, and exact-system arms; require positive and
   negative controls; route a failed positive control to instrument failure;
   and record elicitation competence separately from deployed policy.
3. **The evidence ladder (b11–b16):** D0 knowledge, D1 component skill, D2
   bounded workflow, D3 actor uplift, D4 safeguard robustness, and D5 field
   consequence each receive their own visual rung and named bridge requirement.
4. **Disclosure and review (b17–b20):** replace one dramatic score with a
   multidimensional outcome vector; split restricted and public surfaces;
   quarantine invalid or unsafe studies; and freeze the surviving finding into
   a least-authority dossier with a maximum inference.
5. **Use, expiry, and the null (b21–b24):** scoped decision owners receive the
   packet without a portable label; material change expires it; the exact
   argument-level evidence ceiling remains visible; and a weak instrument is
   prevented from becoming evidence of absent capability.
6. **Handoff (b25):** bounded evidence crosses no command-authority boundary as
   the next chapter opens sensing, command, autonomous action, and strategic
   stability.

## Review obligations

- Rejection crosses must be local to their named object and must not accumulate
  into later scenes.
- The harmless analogue must never imply a hazardous-domain result.
- The D0–D5 ladder must show both the rung identities and the unearned bridges.
- The failed positive-control scene must resolve to instrument failure, not
  incapability.
- The final packet must retain `NO COMMAND AUTHORITY` and the argument-support,
  no-hazardous-result footer.
