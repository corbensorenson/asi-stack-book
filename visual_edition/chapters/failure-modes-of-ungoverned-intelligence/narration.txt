This chapter asks a specific question: Governance, reliability, and self-improvement claims cannot be tested unless their failure conditions, observable boundary events, evidence records, and owners are declared prospectively. The tempting shortcut is insufficient: Labels such as alignment failure or unsafe autonomy do not by themselves identify which contract failed, what observable event occurred, which detector fired, whether an effect escaped, or who owns containment and residuals.
