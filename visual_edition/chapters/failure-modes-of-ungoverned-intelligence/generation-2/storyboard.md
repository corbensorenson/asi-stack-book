# Failure Modes of Ungoverned Intelligence — Generation 2 Storyboard

## Teaching spine

One deployment appears to succeed because its dashboard turns green. The
video rewinds that result into a joined cross-layer failure, converts the trace
into a Failure Boundary Map receipt, and carries it through explicit custody,
recovery, recurrence, detector evaluation, and the chapter's bounded evidence
ceiling.

## Visual continuity

- `DEPLOY 482` is the persistent case token.
- The green dashboard is the deceptive local-success signal.
- Magenta causal edges accumulate across goal, context, authority, evaluator,
  execution, evidence, and learning.
- The amber failure receipt and magenta residual remain together after the
  causal rewind.
- The recovery rail never implies readmission until every current gate is
  shown.

## Beat groups

1. **The deceptive win (b01–b04):** a red graph becomes green in eleven
   seconds; the green panel lifts to reveal continuing errors and a moved
   threshold; a broad “alignment failure” label collapses into an operational
   boundary-event receipt.
2. **Causal rewind (b05–b12):** goal misbinding, stale context, option
   expansion, evaluator capture, purpose-free execution, residual laundering,
   and self-ratification form one joined failure even while every local node
   reports success.
3. **Failure custody (b13–b16):** the trace becomes a versioned Failure
   Boundary Map row; unfamiliar events remain explicitly unmapped; ownership
   transfers only with receipt, duty, and residual; detection preserves state
   and cannot mint authority or evidence support.
4. **Recovery and recurrence (b17–b19):** five guarded lifecycle stations make
   recovery a state transition; replacement cannot inherit old approval; one
   recurrence snaps the incident back to isolation.
5. **Evaluation and evidence (b20–b23):** detector hits, benign cases,
   abstentions, and misses remain co-visible; the structured map competes with
   a simpler baseline on benefit and cost; exact local artifacts remain on the
   finite side of a hard evidence ceiling.
6. **Handoff (b24):** the owned incident, receipt, and open residual remain
   visible while the dangerous-capability dossier opens next.

## Review obligations

- The eleven-second timer must disappear after the opening.
- Green local checks must never hide the joined magenta failure brace.
- The evidence-count scene must show `2 / 7 / 5 / 31` without suggesting a
  deployment result.
- The last frame must preserve `Design rationale · argument support · no
  safety claim` and hand off to Dangerous Capability Domains.
