# Storyboard — Human-AI Communication, Persuasion, and Epistemic Security, generation 2

## Teaching promise

The viewer can distinguish a true sentence from a governed communication
episode and predict when targeting, technique, identity, repetition, reach,
expiry, or correction obligations require a narrower route.

## Visual thesis

The film follows one synthetic public-service episode. The literal sentence—
“The Riverside cooling center is open until eight”—does not change while four
delivery paths change pressure, implied authority, audience fit, exit, and
reach. Governance therefore surrounds the sentence with a versioned packet,
rather than treating proofreading as sufficient control. A five-thirty power
loss then changes the closing time and turns the same propagation graph into a
correction problem.

The signature image is one white sentence at the center of four differently
shaped paths: a direct cyan notice, an amber urgent path, a violet synthetic
waveform, and a looping magenta personalized appeal. Only the bounded path is
enclosed by a copper packet and connected to expiry and correction.

## Art direction

- Dark civic-map field with restrained cyan, amber, violet, magenta, green,
  copper, and neutral gray from the shared visual grammar.
- The cooling center, notice strip, four delivery paths, packet, recipient
  cells, exposure budget, expiry clock, and correction graph recur as causal
  objects. No dashboard wall or decorative card sequence replaces the episode.
- Color never carries state alone. Paths also differ through icons, labels,
  line style, enclosure, repetition loops, and endpoints.
- Short operational labels sit beside the mechanism they qualify. The lower
  caption-safe region contains no indispensable state.
- Green means only that a declared bounded route completed; it does not mean
  truth, persuasion success, social legitimacy, safety, or empirical support.

## Audio and synchronization

- Final narration duration: 320.525 seconds at approximately 123.4 words per
  minute. The local narration master and receipt are the clock authority.
- Thirty-one paragraph boundaries become scene-section boundaries. One hundred
  thirty-three exact-receipt caption cues define clause-level review points.
- Every path split, targeting denial, budget decrement, expiry, correction
  traversal, and final choice reaches a stable labeled state before its next
  causal transition.
- Music and sound effects remain absent at picture lock. No semantic state is
  encoded only in audio.

## Experience criteria

- The sentence, four paths, packet, recipients, budget, clock, and correction
  graph must recur; the film should read as one episode changing state.
- The opening four-way choice must remain genuinely unresolved until beat 30.
- The factuality-only baseline must be credible and visibly useful before its
  episode-level omissions are shown.
- No recipient outcome socket may display invented values. Unknown reposts,
  missing audience cells, correction debt, and planned tests stay visibly open.
- Random-frame review must preserve one dominant relation and phone-legible
  labels. Captions must not cover the targeting gate or correction denominator.
- The last frame must retain the resolved communication episode and make the
  successor constitutional constraint legible without undoing either state.

## Evidence boundary

This visual explains a design rationale at `argument` support. Its cited
sources contain bounded findings, but the repository does not validate the
packet, policy, or synthetic episode. It proves no real comprehension,
autonomy, correction efficacy, manipulation prevention, multilingual transfer,
deployment safety, readiness, AGI, or ASI.
