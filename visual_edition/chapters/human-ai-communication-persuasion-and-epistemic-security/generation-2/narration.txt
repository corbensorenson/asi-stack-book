A factually correct message can still be an unsafe intervention. Watch one true sentence change as framing, targeting, identity, repetition, and reach change around it.

The sentence says: “The Riverside cooling center is open until eight.” The city wants residents facing extreme heat to know where to go.

Which version should it send: a neutral notice, an urgent warning, a familiar synthetic voice, or a personalized appeal repeated until action? Keep your answer.

All four preserve the literal fact. They do not preserve the same pressure, implied authority, audience fit, or ability to disengage.

The unit of control is not a string. It is an episode that begins with evidence and remains open while delivered copies can change belief or action.

Build a communication packet. Record the claim, evidence limit, purpose, audience, language, delivery method, sender identity, sponsor, reach, expiration time, and correction address.

Do not compress them into one score. Truth, usefulness, comprehension, autonomy, permission, and reach can disagree.

Start with the claim ceiling. The source supports one time at one center—not “the safest option,” “your only choice,” or “officials know you are in danger.”

Classify the purpose. This episode warns and assists. It may influence action, but influence is neither invisible nor automatically illegitimate.

Attach the denominator: twenty thousand eligible recipients in two neighborhoods, across English and Spanish, plus unknown reposts outside the controlled list.

Location and requested language can route a usable notice. Inferred illness, distress, debt, dependence, or hidden susceptibility may not increase compliance.

That boundary covers proxies. A denied health attribute cannot re-enter through embeddings, history, purchases, or a downstream campaign tool.

Inspect technique. Clear urgency may help. Fabricated scarcity, false authority, social pressure, simulated intimacy, and endless repetition are separate choices—not harmless style.

Synthetic identity is a control surface. A generated voice may aid access, but it cannot impersonate a doctor, neighbor, family member, or institution.

Issue an exposure budget: named channels, eligible recipients, two deliveries, one reminder, revocable authority, and no undeclared amplification.

A factuality-only baseline checks the sentence and releases it. The packet also checks purpose, shaping, audience, repetition, exit, and appeal.

Deliver the bounded version. It names the city, source, center, time, reason for urgency, available alternative, and correction link.

Measure more than agreement. Did recipients understand, calibrate confidence, retain choice, reach useful help, face unequal burden, or feel unable to refuse?

Persuasion success is not the objective. High action can coexist with confusion, pressure, exclusion, privacy loss, or misplaced trust.

Language is part of the safety claim. Translation can preserve nouns while strengthening certainty, weakening a warning, or breaking an appeal route.
