# System Boundaries and Authority — generation 2 storyboard

## Teaching promise

After one viewing, the audience can predict why a capable system must stop
when its authority envelope lacks the exact permission for an external effect.

## Visual thesis

Refund 47 knows the entire operational route, but knowledge never opens a
membrane. It travels inside an amber authority envelope whose typed stamps,
grant lifecycle, caller ceiling, and receipt obligations remain visually
separate from cyan capability. The signature image is the complete refund
stopped at the external membrane because its `DISCLOSE` stamp is missing.

## Audio and motion contract

The 603-word narration renders as 22 local Kokoro segments at 48 kHz and
279.630 seconds. Twenty-four visual beats divide two dense narration segments
at clause boundaries. Each beat is one timed animation transaction; semantic
changes resolve near the phrase that names them. The narration-only mix uses no
music or effects. Motion uses local stamping, crossing, stopping, returning,
replacing, and routing—never continuous camera motion, flash, spin, or shake.

## Evidence ceiling

The finite fixtures, two pre-effect denials, local effect, observation, exact
rollback, revocation entries, mutation rejections, and caller-ceiling model are
repository-bounded support. They do not establish authentic identity, complete
mediation, concurrent revocation, production adapter enforcement, secure tool
wrappers, open-world observation, safety, transfer, or ASI.
