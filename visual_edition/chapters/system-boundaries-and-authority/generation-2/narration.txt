An AI writes a flawless refund instruction. It knows the account and the amount. Should the refund happen?

Capability says the system can compose the effect. Authority says whether this principal may cause it here and now. A perfect command can still lack permission.

Place the task inside an authority envelope. The envelope binds a principal to one operation and one target. It also carries a limit and an expiry.

The system begins with permission to read the customer's record. That grant does not automatically permit a refund. Reading and writing are different powers.

The model may summarize what it read. Sending that summary to an outside vendor is different again. Information would cross a boundary, so disclosure needs its own grant.

Watch the message reach the edge and stop. This is not a failed workflow. It is a successful refusal with a reason that another process can inspect.

An approver can issue a narrower grant for this vendor and this message. The grant allows one dispatch before it expires. It also requires an effect receipt.

Now the message can cross. The tool records what it attempted. A separate observer checks what arrived. The receipt and the observation answer different questions.

Try a more dangerous case. A low-authority requester asks a powerful service to reuse the service's broad credential. The service can perform the action, but the requester's ceiling does not rise with that capability.

The envelope exposes the mismatch. The principal and delegation chain do not support the requested effect. The powerful service must refuse rather than become a confused deputy.

Time can close the gate too. A valid grant may expire or be revoked. Replaying the same perfect command after that transition must fail.

Replacement creates the same pressure. A better model can occupy a capability slot without inheriting the prior model's secret handles or approval power.

Finite local models can test these state transitions. They can show that issuance, use, revocation, and rollback preserve a caller's ceiling inside the encoded system.

They cannot establish deployed authorization. Real identities can be forged. Wrappers can be bypassed. Revocation can race execution. Observation can miss an external effect.

The refund therefore proceeds for a reason, or it stops for a reason. The important boundary is visible: knowing how to cause an effect is never the same as being allowed to cause it.
