## 02:01–02:47 — Failure boundary

**Visual description.** Four failure cards meet a red fail-closed boundary labeled RECORD THE RESIDUAL.

**Narration.** The design can still fail. Value flattening compresses incommensurable claims, uncertainty, standing, and vetoes into one scalar objective. False consensus treats majority aggregation, model synthesis, silence, exhausted agreement, or one reviewer verdict as settled moral agreement. Stakeholder erasure omits affected parties, future people, third parties, or less powerful participants from standing, evidence, notice, and appeal. Procedure laundering hides a contested moral choice inside an apparently neutral aggregation rule, threshold, priority, taxonomy, or default. The correct response is to stop, narrow, quarantine, compensate, or retain an owned residual rather than narrate uncertainty away.
