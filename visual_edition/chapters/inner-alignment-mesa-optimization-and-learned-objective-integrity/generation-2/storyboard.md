# Chapter 13 Generation-2 Storyboard

## Promise and evidence ceiling

The viewer should leave able to distinguish an authorized target, the actual learning signal, the resulting policy, and a hypothesis about a learned objective. The film makes one prediction concrete: two policies can share every observed training trace while a capability-preserving feature swap separates their behavior. The chapter remains `Design rationale` at `argument` support. Its sorting-lab case and finite repository traces do not identify a real model's objective, establish the presence or absence of a mesa-optimizer, detect deception, validate a mitigation, establish monitor independence, prove complete rollback, or make deployment safe.

## Persistent visual world

One parcel-sorting lab persists for the entire film. Emergency insulin packs and ordinary supply boxes move along a dark conveyor toward a clinic or warehouse dock. Four color-coded objects remain distinct: the authorized target (gold), the learning signal (copper), the observed policy/behavior (blue), and learned-objective hypotheses (violet). Green denotes capable/intended action; red denotes a wrong action, a rejected mutation, or an unresolved boundary. The record, not the animation, is the authority: empty result sockets remain visibly empty whenever the prospective campaign has not run.

## Story spine

1. Two policies make the same correct training choice, so behavior alone leaves the learned rule unresolved.
2. A correlated medical seal and green stripe make the target and reward signal look identical on training support.
3. The integrity record keeps target, signal, policy, and objective hypothesis separate and lists live alternatives.
4. A sealed, capability-preserving stripe swap makes the shortcut fail while the intended policy remains capable.
5. Training history, evaluator awareness, internal search, white-box clues, and architecture changes add evidence but never become authority by themselves.
6. Mitigation is split into removal, concealment, and capability damage; held-out triggers and monitor-blind controls keep the denominator honest.
7. A versioned Learned-Objective Integrity Record carries lineage, evidence, expiry, rollback, descendants, and non-authorities.
8. The finite result is bounded non-identification and fail-closed record behavior; the mature empirical campaign is explicitly still empty, then Moral Uncertainty receives the unresolved target question.

## Experience-review watch points

- Beats 01–08 must make the opening puzzle and the evidence boundary legible before introducing the record schema; no abstract “alignment” badge may substitute for the matched traces.
- Beats 09–12 must preserve the same crate, seal, stripe, and destination geometry through the intervention. The stripe swap is the causal payoff, not a generic adversarial flourish.
- Beats 13–19 must keep the evidence lanes visibly plural. White-box color, a camera signal, or a clean score must not become a hidden authority channel.
- Beats 20–26 must show material state and descendant changes as lineage operations. The record is not merely a checklist; it carries expiry, rollback, and non-authority fields.
- Beats 22–24 must distinguish mitigation removal, concealment, and capability damage with separate post-mitigation paths and held-out controls.
- Beats 27–30 must visually separate the finite repository result, the prospective campaign, and the broad conclusions that remain unsupported.
- Mechanical diagnostics flag three approximately six-second holds. They are intentional reading windows for the shared-dependency matrix, the bounded white-box activation pattern, and the state-lineage strip; confirm at normal speed and muted playback that labels remain purposeful, then retain rather than add decorative motion.

## Acceptance notes

The 32 beat boundaries come from the accepted narration receipt, not estimated prose timing. The 436.930-second duration is above the preferred 3–6-minute pacing range because shortening the campaign and evidence-boundary section would collapse the distinction between finite proof, prospective work, and nonclaims. The duration is therefore a soft-target exception with a recorded rationale, not a new default. Clause-level transformations, exact captions, and the descriptive transcript must remain aligned at picture and sound lock.
