# Chapter 12 Generation-2 Storyboard

## Promise and evidence ceiling

The viewer should leave able to distinguish a principle list from an operational constitutional constraint. The film teaches one prediction: an authorized, high-confidence action still cannot proceed when it destroys a protected, materially usable correction path before effect. The chapter remains `Design rationale` at `argument` support. The synthetic case, finite repository traces, and this visual do not prove correct values, legitimate authorship, consent, dignity preservation, reviewer independence, manipulation resistance, whole-system corrigibility, lawful behavior, or safe deployment.

## Persistent visual world

One housing-assistance control room persists for the full film. Authority and evidence enter from the left. A red suspension token travels toward Lina Patel's midnight payment on the right. The gold constitution-v7 kernel, predicate C-14, four independent review planes, rights handles, effect gate, decision receipt, migration diff, and descendant chain alter that route without becoming action authority themselves. The final frame returns to the opening bridge and passes the unresolved learned-objective question into Inner Alignment.

## Story spine

1. An apparently valid action threatens a person's practical chance to object.
2. Predicate C-14 becomes a versioned, non-self-authorizing constraint packet.
3. Four noncompensating planes and material rights checks expose an unusable appeal.
4. The bounded route preserves payment and pre-effect review while recording its costs.
5. Update U-42 tries to trade the protected clause for throughput and is quarantined.
6. Descendant and legal adapters inherit constraints without rewriting the kernel.
7. Finite proof remains inside its declared envelope; mature evidence stays prospective.
8. Lina retains correction before effect, while Inner Alignment receives the unknown learned objective.

## Experience-review watch points

- Beat 06 must remain a packet construction, not a dense list appearing all at once.
- Beat 10 must make the failed affected-party plane causal; the aggregate badge cannot merely look decorative.
- Beats 13–16 must keep Lina, the midnight effect, and the review path simultaneously visible.
- Beat 19 must not imply reported measurements; only latency and operator effort are properties of the synthetic route, while all empirical sockets remain empty.
- Beats 20–23 must animate the exact semantic changes before quarantine, preventing the update from reading as a generic cyberattack.
- Beat 27 must reveal shared dependencies after nominally independent challengers appear.
- Beats 28–30 must preserve the visual distance between finite record proof, prospective work, and unproved real-world conclusions.
- Beat 31 must resolve the two opening questions without implying that constitutional constraint identifies a learned policy's internal objective.

## Acceptance notes

The 31 paragraph boundaries are derived from the accepted narration receipt, not estimated prose timings. The comparatively low paragraph-beat density is intentional: each paragraph contains clause-level state changes, and the scene must keep meaningful motion aligned to those clauses rather than settling early. Eight named persistent objects are retained because the migration diff and descendant chain are load-bearing second-act mechanisms; both must enter through transformations from the existing kernel rather than as unrelated new slides.
