A result card arrives with a confident headline: “The memory adapter improved retrieval by eighteen percent.” A green stamp lands on it: PROVEN. Stop there. What, exactly, was proven?

The sentence is only a claim atom. Its wording, scope, and version must stay fixed while we ask what evidence actually touched it.

Four identities must remain separate. The claim label says what kind of assertion this is. Support state says what backs it. Lifecycle state says where review stands. Disposition says what ultimately happened.

Now split the atom into three synchronized views: the proposition a reader sees, the obligation the architecture imposes, and the predicate a machine can check. Substituting any one creates a different claim.

Evidence enters with a role, not a halo. A citation can supply lineage or context. A proof can cover a formal predicate. A fixture can test one encoded behavior. A measurement can estimate an effect.

Store those artifacts in a non-aggregating evidence cell. Independence, reproducibility, recency, coverage, adversarial strength, validity, artifact access, and transfer distance remain separate dimensions.

Imagine seven dimensions look excellent, but the evaluator is the same system that produced the answer. Independence remains weak. Seven bright bars cannot average that missing safeguard away.

To move support, construct a transition packet. Bind the exact claim version, source passage or artifact, evidence role, scope, method, negative results, reviewer, blockers, downgrade triggers, limitations, and explicit non-claims.

The packet approaches an upward gate. It opens only when the target support state names its required evidence and every non-compensable requirement is present.

A nearby citation does not open it. The source may motivate the mechanism while saying nothing about this adapter, workload, effect size, or implementation.

A proof object does not open every gate either. It can establish the encoded theorem under its assumptions; it cannot silently become deployment, usefulness, or real-world enforcement evidence.

A benchmark score is also scoped. It speaks for the frozen tasks, protocol, comparator, and measurement boundary—not every user, distribution, system state, or future revision.

Return to the eighteen-percent headline. Version one claims a held-out retrieval gain for a named adapter, model, corpus, metric, baseline, and confidence procedure. The scope now fits on the card.

A passage-reviewed paper supports the adapter’s design rationale. Record that role. It does not measure our implementation, so the effect claim remains unmoved.

A synthetic fixture verifies that the adapter routes the authored cases correctly. That earns narrow synthetic-test backing for the fixture claim, not an eighteen-percent empirical gain.

The independent held-out run is still missing. The empirical gate remains closed, and the public ledger records the blocker instead of replacing it with optimistic prose.

Then a positive control fails. The instrument could not detect a known retrieval difference. That result does not prove no improvement; it weakens the test and forces the inference downward.

Transitions are not only promotions. Review may leave support unchanged, narrow scope, downgrade backing, refute the atom, or deprecate a superseded version.

Negative evidence stays attached like a visible shadow. A cleaner revision may answer it, but it may not delete the failed control, contradiction, or abandoned inference from lineage.

Appendix C is a public projection of this ledger. It shows current claim, support, transition, limitation, and residual. The projection is an interface—not another piece of evidence.

Nor does one accepted transition move a whole claim family. Parent, sibling, and descendant claims keep their own identities and burdens unless separately reviewed.

The decision record preserves reviewer independence, dissent, acceptance blockers, limitations, and the reason for no change or movement. Confidence changes through receipts, not mood.

This is not a numeric confidence machine. Evidence dimensions answer different questions, and the public support state is a lossy summary chosen for a particular claim and reader.

This chapter remains Design rationale at argument support. Its schemas, finite proofs, and synthetic mutation suites establish only their encoded record and transition behavior; they do not validate the method in deployment.

The payoff is disciplined revision. Clearer prose may improve understanding, but only an accepted, claim-specific transition may strengthen what the book says the evidence supports.
