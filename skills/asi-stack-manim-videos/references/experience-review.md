## Review invariants

A review is evidence about one exact artifact. Bind every artifact relevant at
that stage and make unavailable downstream artifacts explicitly null. Record
reviewer identity, role, prior exposure, allowed materials,
authorship/implementation involvement, raw responses, timestamps, and
unresolved defects. A summary without raw evidence cannot pass.

Prepare every review as a four-artifact custody set: the exact context manifest,
the prompt delivered in that context, the unassessed raw response, and the
review record created afterward. Bind all four by digest and session ID. A
fresh-session declaration without those artifacts is insufficient. Never edit
the raw response after assessment; append corrections or interpretation only
to the review record.

Do not average away a weak dimension. Do not let schema validity, a successful
render, or an aesthetically polished frame substitute for full-speed viewing.
Do not call an AI proxy a human learner or an external reviewer.

## Gate 0: treatment and script

Review the evidence contract, audience, promise alternatives, selected case,
medium, story form and basis, macro moves, keyframes, narration, payoff, transfer
criterion, package promise, and accessibility plan before expensive work.

Pass only when:

- the selected promise is one consequential and observable transfer;
- every claim and visual inference has an authority reference;
- assumptions, simplifications, notation, units, and non-claims are explicit;
- every macro move names a prior model, a visible event, and a changed model;
- keyframes make each mental-model change visible;
- the title/thumbnail promise begins delivery inside 15 seconds;
- read-aloud, truth, and visualizability reviews pass on the exact narration;
- every sentence has a meaningful visual or deliberate audio-only role; and
- no open treatment or script defect remains.

The narration linter is one input. It is not this gate.

## Gate 1: animatic

Use rough geometry, near-final performance-block audio, and at least block-timed
beats. The implementation diagnostic may know the script and code. It must
still judge the rendered artifact rather than implementation intent.

Bind the draft sandbox receipt and its exact narration-bearing animatic mux.
The silent visual track is an intermediate and cannot be reviewed as an
animatic. Captions, transcript, thumbnail, A/V diagnostic, and final render
receipt do not exist at this gate and must be null rather than fabricated.
Run the auditor's ephemeral `--animatic-preflight` on the canonical mux to
locate freezes, black intervals, silence, loudness, peak, stream, and duration
risks before playback. Its stdout is a repair aid, not the governed final A/V
artifact and not evidence that animatic review passed. It reports a scoped
`mechanical_result`, never the governed diagnostic's `validation_state`.

Pass normal-speed, muted, audio-only, phone, and random-frame review. Mark
captions-on as not applicable because final captions do not yet exist. Confirm
promise, story, persistent world, focal hierarchy, continuity, rhythm, local
evidence boundary, and payoff. Rewrite freely. Do not polish weak geometry or
transitions instead of repairing the model.

For the animatic phone pass, play the complete candidate at a recorded
phone-equivalent viewport no wider than 360 CSS pixels without zoom. Every
label that carries a unique identity, state, comparison, limit, or causal step
must remain readable when it matters. Consolidate fields or enlarge the active
relation when narration would otherwise have to rescue microtext. Record the
viewport and playback method; inspecting desktop frames or a scaled-up contact
sheet is not a phone pass.
The recorded method and physical-device flag must agree.

## Gate 2: picture and sound lock

Use final geometry, manually reviewed forced alignment, narration, captions,
transcript, easing, camera, voice, mix, and consequential sound. Require all
viewing modes. Inspect actual captions over the final mux, not a simulated
caption box. Check every pronunciation and regenerated splice. Bind the exact
release-profile sandbox receipt, A/V diagnostic, and compiler-derived render
receipt. The thumbnail remains outside this gate and must be null.

Lock means later changes are expensive; it does not mean publication-ready.
Any media, timing, caption, or transcript change reopens this gate.

## Gate 3A: source-aware release critic

The critic must not have authored the script or implemented the scene. Supply:

- rendered master, captions, transcript, sandbox and final receipts, A/V
  diagnostic, and sample manifest;
- canonical chapter and source notes;
- treatment, truth checks, beat plan, and visual simplifications; and
- the claim/evidence ceiling and non-claims.

Run the critic in a fresh AI task or independent human session. The context
manifest must include exactly the supplied chapter, source notes, treatment,
beat plan, narration, scene, captions, transcript, and rendered candidate,
with no prior review. Preserve its raw timestamped observations before scoring.

The critic checks every spoken claim, visual inference, qualifier, source end
card, and package promise. Claim fidelity must score at least 4/5 with
timestamped evidence. A beautiful but overbroad metaphor fails.

## Gate 3B: cold audience proxy

Use a different reviewer with no prior exposure and no role in authorship or
implementation. Quarantine context. Supply only the rendered video, captions,
and optionally the descriptive transcript. Do not supply the chapter, sources,
script, treatment, beat plan, scene code, prior review, expected answer, or
answer key.

Start a new task or independent human session with no inherited artifact
conversation. Freeze the context manifest and prompt before viewing. The raw
response file must contain the comprehension and transfer answers before the
assessor sees or applies the success criteria. Keep assessment language out of
the prompt and raw-response artifact.

Before viewing, record:

- a comprehension prompt about the mechanism in the shown case;
- a transfer prompt that changes one material condition;
- observable success criteria for each; and
- an assessor who will compare raw answers to those criteria.

After viewing, preserve raw responses before assessment. Pass only when both
responses meet the predeclared criteria and required viewing modes pass. A
wrong or ungrounded confident answer reopens the treatment, script, or visual
model. The proxy must not score source-aware claim fidelity.

A generic correct answer is not enough. At least one comprehension or transfer
response must use a concrete visual cue or causal transition from the reviewed
artifact. Record its timestamp, the observed cue, an exact excerpt already
present in the raw response, and why that cue supports the answer. If the
answer would pass without engaging the artifact, mark artifact grounding failed.
This control improves diagnostic attribution; it still does not prove the
video caused learning or exclude every contribution from prior knowledge.

State the limit in every record: this is a diagnostic proxy result for one
artifact, not evidence of human learning, retention, or population effects.

## Sampling and playback

Create a manifest with start, quarter, midpoint, three-quarter, and end samples
for every beat. Add samples around rapid transitions, camera reframes,
occlusions, label crossings, and suspicious easing. Bind the manifest digest,
expected beat count, sampled beat count, and unresolved interpolation count.
Use one set for the exact animatic identity and one set for the exact final
master/plan identity. The latter may be bound unchanged into lock, source-aware,
and cold reviews; do not re-extract identical frames merely to rename a lane.

Contact sheets inspect composition and intermediate state. They do not replace
motion or sound review. Watch:

- complete video at 1x with audio;
- muted for visible mechanism and state;
- audio-only for coherent language and voice;
- captions-on for sync, line breaks, reading load, and active-region clearance;
- phone and large screen for hierarchy, typography, edges, and comfort;
- headphones and ordinary speakers for voice, silence, effects, and mix; and
- random frames for accidental states.

All modes are required at lock and source-aware release. The cold proxy must at
least pass normal-speed, captions-on, phone, and random-frame modes. A pass
record may mark a mode not applicable only when the gate contract does not
require it and the note gives a concrete reason.

At picture-and-sound lock and release, the phone pass uses an ordinary physical
phone for complete playback, including captions and sound. A desktop viewport
is a useful preflight but does not establish physical-device legibility,
caption clearance, thermal or playback behavior, or speaker intelligibility.

## Score anchors

Attach a rationale and at least one evidence timestamp to every applicable
score. Use these anchors consistently:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | materially prevents understanding or creates serious discomfort/error |
| 2 | repeated major defects; intended mechanism is difficult to recover |
| 3 | understandable with noticeable repair work or avoidable cognitive load |
| 4 | clear, deliberate, comfortable, and release-worthy with no material defect |
| 5 | unusually effective; each major decision reinforces the promise without excess |

Score these dimensions independently:

| Dimension | Acceptance question |
|---|---|
| teaching clarity | Can the reviewer explain or predict the promised mechanism? |
| composition | Does each state have legible hierarchy and breathing room? |
| motion quality | Is motion smooth, meaningful, apprehensible, and comfortable? |
| synchronization | Do visual onset and resolution match the spoken idea? |
| continuity | Can persistent identity and state be tracked without re-decoding? |
| pacing | Do construction, movement, pause, and reveal form a coherent rhythm? |
| voice | Is delivery intelligible, natural, varied, and splice-free? |
| sound mix | Is speech effortless on every required device? |
| engagement | Does honest tension sustain attention and receive payoff? |
| accessibility | Do captions, description, contrast, redundancy, and comfort pass? |
| claim fidelity | Is every inference inside the chapter's evidence ceiling? |

A passing lane requires at least 4 in every dimension it owns. Record `null`,
an explanatory rationale, and no evidence timestamps for every dimension that
the lane does not own. Do not manufacture a score.

| Lane | Dimensions owned |
|---|---|
| animatic | teaching clarity, composition, motion quality, synchronization, continuity, pacing, voice, engagement |
| picture and sound lock | all dimensions except claim fidelity |
| source-aware release | all dimensions |
| cold audience proxy | all dimensions except claim fidelity |

The cold proxy and implementation diagnostics do not own source-aware claim
fidelity. The animatic also does not own final sound-mix or accessibility
judgment because its final mux and captions do not yet exist.

## Defects and decisions

For each defect, record severity, timestamp or beat, observed viewer problem,
owner, disposition, and rationale when accepted. A passing review has no open
defect. Only a genuinely minor defect may be accepted with a specific rationale;
critical or major defects must be resolved.

For revisions, compare predecessor and candidate at matched playback volume,
display size, and promise. Keep a change when it resolves a named problem.
Return to the earlier gate when the defect belongs to selection, truth, story,
or visual model rather than implementation polish.

After publication, retention and comments can suggest where to inspect. They
do not establish learning. Record a hypothesis before editing, preserve the
predecessor, and rerun every invalidated gate.
