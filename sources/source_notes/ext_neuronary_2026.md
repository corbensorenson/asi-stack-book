## Thesis

Share small MLP-neuron intervention recipes with model names, doses and example outputs.

## Mechanisms

The format records coordinates, add/multiply semantics, hook site, normalization, token scope and optional generation/example metadata. Its runner uses forward pre-hooks with residual-norm/down-column scaling for additive edits; schema and runner capabilities are separate.

## Evidence

The site provides community recipes and reproduction/interpretation/readout channels. A crowdsourced example or reproduction report is a research lead rather than a certified causal result.

## Failure Modes

A model repository name is not necessarily an exact checkpoint digest. Schema acceptance is not engine support: the inspected runner rejects MoE expert edits and does not implement free-form conditional recipes. Submitted interpretation/confidence or votes do not establish unique feature semantics, alignment or independent reproduction.
