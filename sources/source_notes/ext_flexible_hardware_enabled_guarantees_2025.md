# Flexible Hardware-Enabled Guarantees for AI Compute

Source ID: `ext_flexible_hardware_enabled_guarantees_2025`
Source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15093>

The report proposes an auditable guarantee processor plus a tamper-resistant
enclosure to verify or enforce updateable claims about accelerator use while
protecting sensitive information. It is a design proposal, not a deployed
guarantee. Trust depends on hardware and firmware integrity, update authority,
coverage of legacy and uninstrumented compute, side channels, governance
legitimacy, privacy, and resistance to abuse.
