# Are Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models a Mirage?

Source ID: `ext_emergent_abilities_mirage_2023`
Primary source: <https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/adc98a266f45005c403b8311ca7e8bd7-Abstract-Conference.html>

Schaeffer et al. show in studied settings that discontinuous metrics and small
test sets can make smooth changes appear abruptly emergent. The book uses this
as a mandatory competing explanation and a requirement for continuous,
probabilistic, and uncertainty-aware measurements. It does not prove that every
capability transition is a metric artifact.
