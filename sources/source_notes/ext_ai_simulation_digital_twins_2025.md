# AI Simulation by Digital Twins

Source ID: `ext_ai_simulation_digital_twins_2025`
Source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06580>

The systematic survey and reference framework position digital twins as
virtual representations linked to physical systems for simulation, training,
monitoring, and decision support. The book uses this to require explicit
virtual/physical synchronization, fidelity, latency, coverage, and sim-to-real
residuals. A twin is not automatically faithful or safe for policy transfer.

Routing: Embodied Agency, Real-Time Control, and Physical Safety.
