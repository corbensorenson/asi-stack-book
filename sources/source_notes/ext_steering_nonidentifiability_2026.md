# On the Non-Identifiability of Steering Vectors in Large Language Models

Sohan Venkatesh, Ashish Mahendran Kurapath. [Primary paper, arXiv 2602.06801v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06801v4).

## Mechanism and evidence

Challenge the identity of a proposed steering direction using controlled perturbations and matched controls.

The authors found similar semantic scores for perturbed steering vectors across tested traits and prompts.

## Reviewed passage and limits

Figure 1 and finite-strength perturbation experiments. Equality under a local first-order map is a mathematical condition. Finite interventions and new prompt distributions require behavioral checks.
