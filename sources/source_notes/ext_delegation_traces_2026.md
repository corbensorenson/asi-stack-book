# Following Delegation Traces to Study Human Oversight in Interaction with Multimodal AI

| Field | Value |

Chiatti, Matteucci and Schiaffonati (2026). [Primary publication](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21948v1).

## Thesis

A person's stated willingness to verify is observable separately from their confidence in the model.

## Mechanisms

Measure trust and the chosen future delegation policy separately. To establish enacted oversight, inspect a decision and the independent observation on which it depended.

## Evidence

Fifty-six AI-literate participants contributed seven video-question trials each. Intended independent verification was common even at high reported trust.

## Failure Modes

Sections 3–5, especially Table 1 and the response options. The 392 trials are repeated observations. The future policy was self-reported, and the proposed study of enacted delegation is prospective.
