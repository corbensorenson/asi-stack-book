## Thesis and evidence boundary

A map of conditional computational capabilities can be a useful evidence and accounting instrument without being a better controller for discovering systems. The paper synthesizes a completed program with bounded positive measurements, model repairs, failed advance rules, imperfect controls and a post hoc correction. It is not a synthesis derived from the ASI Stack book, and does not independently corroborate its own underlying research papers.

| Stage | Source-reported observation | Boundary to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations and pilots | Exact finite checks; restricted compilation benefits; representation-dependent storage reversal; timing-boundary ranking differences | Declared grammars and one shared desktop; established mechanisms; no unrestricted optimality, generality tax or energy result |
| Phase 1 | Atlas minus factorized engineering -0.005783, 95% interval [-0.009672, -0.001838], 32 worlds; separate family extension also favors factorization | Particular grammar, score, policies and protocol; exploratory ablation hint does not reproduce in direction |
| Maintenance | Work-count selector minus generic-regression excess cost +0.003306, interval [-0.025998, +0.029082], eight worlds | No clear advantage or equivalence; interrupted total work remains unknown |
| Phase 2 | Routing controller loses to matched no-diagnosis arm at all five coupling levels; aggregate coverage passes while high-coupling coverage fails | Selected probes can expose real interactions without paying for their opportunity cost; shuffle left relevant tags unchanged |
| Phase 3 | Revised recipe gains +0.014955 at once-only development validation; 80% interval [+0.007198, +0.023026] | Misses .02 point margin and spending screen; confirmation unrun; supplied-library selection is not primitive invention |
| Phase 3.1 | Particular transport and affordability controls fail to explain away every gain | Same inspected training worlds, ordinary descriptive intervals; no unique diagnostic causation or equivalence |
| Phase 3.2a | Adding buy-all reduces paid-key-policy headroom at budget 128 to +0.035299, interval [+0.021985, +0.048655]; stop under the fixed rule | Same 24 worlds; explicitly post hoc; interval includes .04; imperfect oracle policy is not a mathematical upper bound |

Independent N is worlds, with fixed seed conditions averaged within worlds. Event counts, integrity repeats, mappings and budgets do not enlarge N. Raw replay, independent replication, public reproducibility and external review are distinct from the source's internal checks.

## Citation-ready paraphrases

These are proposed paraphrases for the book, not new experimental claims:

1. "The Answerability Atlas experiments distinguish organizing evidence about computational capabilities from demonstrating a better policy for discovering them. Their measurement apparatus was useful, but the tested discovery controllers did not establish an advantage over strong alternatives."
2. "In the final corrected screen, a policy given the useful component pair and charged four budget units for that information retained a positive point advantage, but missed the study's practical continuation threshold once buying all components was included as a comparator."
3. "The program illustrates why an intelligence architecture should distinguish prediction, experiment selection, component acquisition, composition and deployment. Success at one stage is not sufficient evidence of success at the next."

Attribute the first two to the project with their study scope. Identify the third as an architectural inference from the evidence, not a verified theorem about intelligence.

## What the book should not claim from this source

- The atlas discovered a new general law of intelligence, a universal resource frontier, or a necessary quantitative generality tax.
- Diagnosis never helps, or the true final headroom has been proved smaller than four score points. The interval includes that value; the stipulated rule failed.
- Correct diagnostic labels caused all, none, or a fixed percentage of the observed gain. Shuffle-gain ratios are not causal percentages.
- The controller invented primitives, learned a general representation language, solved reliable composition, or demonstrated recursive self-improvement.
- Every stage was preregistered confirmation, or all three phases were registered negative trials. Phase 3 stopped during development and the final correction is post hoc.
- The synthetic scores measure physical energy, unrestricted computational optimality, general intelligence, or the probability of ASI.
- Millions of event records, repeated seed conditions, repeated mappings or integrity reruns are independent experimental samples.
- Private backups, automated checks, assistant review or a polished PDF constitute public reproducibility or external peer review.

## Read the final version of each claim

Phase 1's final comparison remains a scoped loss to factorized engineering. Phase 2's aggregate calibration pass remains true, while later work rejects the stronger per-level calibration reading. Phase 3's modest positive development signal remains true, together with no-go and unrun confirmation. The six-comparator headroom proceed is superseded in interpretation by the eight-comparator stop.
