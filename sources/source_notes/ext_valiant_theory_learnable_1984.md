# A Theory of the Learnable

Source ID: `ext_valiant_theory_learnable_1984`
Primary source: <https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1968.1972>

Valiant's PAC formulation makes a learning claim conditional on a concept or
hypothesis class, data-generating assumptions, target error, confidence, and
resource bound. The book uses it to force sample-complexity claims to expose
their quantifiers and assumptions. It does not directly explain modern
foundation-model behavior, distribution shift, in-context learning, or safety,
and it certifies no trained ASI Stack model.

Routing: `learning-theory-generalization-and-scaling-science`.
