# Aligning AI Teams

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Source ID | `ext_aligning_ai_teams_2026` |

Siddarth Srinivasan, Morgan Jane Matthews, Jascha Sohl-Dickstein, Erik Jones. [Primary paper, arXiv 2611.17820v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17820v1).

## Thesis

Group communication can leave responsibility for a constraint unassigned.

## Mechanisms

Compare team-wide responsibility assignments against group chat and a single agent on the same deliverable.

## Evidence

In two software-engineering cases and ten consulting-proposal tasks, critique or group chat did not reliably close the single-agent versus team safety gap. Explicit responsibility or a shared specification helped in some settings.

## Failure Modes

Team interventions and per-task results. Results varied by model and task. Count work completed, adverse outcomes and total inference spent together.
