## Failure Modes

- Decoder, environment, residual, fallback, or reviewer burden is hidden outside the compression denominator.
- Parameter similarity or reconstruction rate stands in for consumer-relative behavior.
- A route is cherry-picked from successful cases while failures, escalation, and migration are omitted.
- Cost pressure erases protected verification, rights, recovery, or human-capacity floors.
