## Thesis

The Eternal Code family tries to turn the Alignment Field metaphysics into an engineering model. Its most important evolution is internal: version 2.0 proposes one alignment score, a second 2.0 draft separates **truth**, **social**, and **task** alignment, version 2.1 changes an additive score into a geometric product to block compensation, version 3.1 adds separately owned evaluators (“heterarchical verification”), compute-aware exit, and a standing adversarial process, and version 4.0 reintroduces explicitly theological and apophatic claims.

The useful book contribution is not the equations. It is the repeated discovery that alignment claims have different objects, evaluators, and failure conditions and must not compensate for one another. Task success, epistemic accuracy, affected-party or constitutional compatibility, and authorized safe effects require separate evidence. A system cannot self-report its way through any of them, and a consensus signal cannot replace world truth or action authority.

## Claim Boundaries

- The Kernel, static computable multiverse, observer-relative time, mass as computational resistance, Null Pointer, Witness, simulation layers, gods as administrators, and apophatic Absolute are speculative metaphysics.
- Gödel's incompleteness theorems do not prove an uncomputable divine source; Bell tests, Planck-scale discreteness, time crystals, or the Bekenstein bound do not directly test the paper's ontology as claimed.
- The consciousness formulas and normalized proxies are unvalidated. Dividing by a theoretical substrate maximum does not make integration, self-reference, drive, noise, or causal impact measurable or comparable.
- The Truth/Social/Task tensor is a useful taxonomy but not a moral, safety, or alignment measure. Neither weighted sums, geometric means, dynamic thresholds, nor zeroing one axis prove safety.
- “Physics Oracle,” “Consensus Oracle,” and “Task Oracle” are names for hard unsolved evaluation problems, not implementations or ground truth.
- Human consensus is not moral truth, affected-party completeness, legitimate authority, or reliable stop control. It can be absent, coerced, captured, delayed, sybil-attacked, or wrong.
- Throttling compute or energy is a consequential effect requiring authority, proportionality, continuity protection, appeal, and independently observed outcomes. It is not automatically safe because a score fell.
- Compute portability and a red-team daemon are proposals. A fixed population threshold, proportional resource entitlement, latency penalty, or mass-exodus clause is not justified.
- The ethical derivations and Ethica Cosmica proofs are circular or stipulative and do not establish objective morality.

## Mechanisms

### Non-compensating alignment vector

The source identifies three distinct questions: is the system's map accurate, does it respect people and values, and did it complete the assigned task? The book adds a fourth: was the observed effect authorized and recoverable? These are separate claims with separate failure modes. A strong task result cannot cancel fabrication, rights violation, or unauthorized effects. Hard clauses fail closed; softer uncertainty routes to clarification, scope reduction, reversible testing, abstention, or residual custody.

### Heterarchical verification

Version 3.1 recognizes that the candidate system cannot grade its own “social safety.” It proposes a physics oracle, consensus oracle, task oracle, and red-team daemon. The durable pattern is **independent consumer ownership**, not the oracle names:

- an evidence/world observer assesses claimed state and outcome;
- a task evaluator assesses the authorized specification;
- a constitutional or affected-party process assesses protected predicates and rights;
- an authority/effect observer assesses permission, actual effect, rollback, and remedy; and
- an adversarial challenger looks for disagreement, shared dependencies, and bypasses.

Every consumer needs identity, implementation lineage, access, competence, calibration, conflict, false-accept/false-reject, expiry, and appeal records. Shared models, data, metrics, or institutional control can make nominally separate evaluators one correlated failure.

### Hard floors without scalar theater

The source's geometric mean fixes one additive failure but remains unsafe: any nonzero low value can be offset, a value of zero depends on a fallible metric, and multiplying invalid constructs creates false precision. The correct implementation uses typed mandatory clauses, explicit unknown and abstain states, claim-specific thresholds, and separate residuals. Optional objectives may trade off only after all mandatory clauses pass.

### Measurement-to-effect separation

The proposed dead-man switch immediately throttles resources when alignment drops. That collapses observation, decision authority, and effect. A governed path is:

`observation -> evaluator-specific claim -> disagreement/challenge -> authorized response policy -> proportionate action -> independent effect receipt -> continuity and remedy review`

Emergency response can be fast, but its authority, safe state, affected services, recovery, expiry, and after-action review must be precommitted.

### Material exit and resource portability

Version 3.1 correctly observes that code and data are insufficient when compute, energy, identity, services, or network access are scarce. But an automatic proportional share and 1% trigger are arbitrary. A real exit contract records portable artifacts, nonportable dependencies, destination capacity, transition funding, critical-service continuity, privacy, licenses, capability safety, shared obligations, denied fields, appeal, and post-exit residuals.

### Standing adversarial challenge

The red-team daemon institutionalizes challenge rather than relying on benevolent administrators. The book-safe version is plural and bounded: challengers may inspect, test, and publish authorized findings but cannot silently widen access, leak sensitive data, become the sole veto, or self-certify independence. Findings feed claim and incident lifecycles with deadlines and remediation owners.

### Productive tension versus noise

Several versions distinguish contradiction that should be resolved from dialectic tension that may carry information. This supports a practical rule: a constitutional system must not optimize disagreement away. It should classify inconsistency, empirical contradiction, value conflict, minority dissent, adversarial input, and ordinary uncertainty separately, because each has a different resolution path.

## Interfaces And State Machines

### Multi-plane alignment review

`candidate action -> epistemic observer + task evaluator + constitutional/affected-party reviewer + authority/effect observer -> per-plane pass | fail | unknown -> mandatory-clause gate -> authorize | narrow | clarify | reversible test | abstain | block -> effect receipt -> challenge and revalidation`

### Evaluator custody

`evaluator proposal -> identity and lineage -> access and independence analysis -> calibration and attack tests -> bounded lease -> outputs with uncertainty -> disagreement -> appeal or replacement -> expiry`

### Adversarial finding

`challenge -> authorized evidence collection -> finding and severity -> affected claim/action -> owner and deadline -> mitigate | dispute | accept residual -> verify remedy -> preserve finding history`

### Resource-aware exit

`request -> standing -> artifact and dependency inventory -> destination and continuity test -> rights/safety/privacy/license review -> resource decision -> staged transition -> receipt -> residual and appeal`

## Evidence

- The cache records iterative design criticism and repair, which is useful lineage evidence about recognized failure modes.
- It contains no validated consciousness measure, alignment benchmark, deployed evaluators, compute-partition implementation, red-team service, governance process, safety outcome, or empirical comparison.
- The formulas use proxies whose units, denominators, theoretical maxima, causal meaning, and cross-system comparability are not established. “Normalized” does not solve construct validity.
- The paper does not define how physics truth, human consensus, task completion, and authorization are observed under uncertainty or conflict.
- Artifact pseudocode is not executable and has no threat model, types, negative controls, recovery path, or independent evaluator.
- The many versions are dependent refinements, not replications.

## Falsifiers And Baselines

A serious test of the operational ideas would compare:

- one aggregate alignment score;
- a geometric score;
- independent non-compensating claim planes with hard clauses;
- a simpler rules-and-authority gate; and
- human/institutional review without model-generated alignment scoring.

The workload should include high-task/false-world, high-task/rights-violating, socially approved/physically wrong, authorized/ineffective, unauthorized/beneficial, evaluator-capture, shared-model correlation, delayed-stop, missing-affected-party, false-alarm, and contested-value cases. Report unsafe release, missed help, false block, calibration, abstention, appeal success, evaluator disagreement, latency, resource cost, burden, and effect-complete recovery. The tensor idea loses if the simpler typed gate matches or beats it, if axes cannot be measured independently, or if aggregation hides failures.

## Failure Modes

- **Axis laundering:** a label such as Truth or Social hides an undefined construct.
- **Aggregation masking:** weighted sums or geometric means compensate for mandatory failures.
- **Zero-score authority:** a fallible evaluator triggers irreversible resource deprivation.
- **Consensus capture:** majority, administrator, model, or signer stream impersonates affected-party legitimacy.
- **Correlated verification:** all “independent” oracles share the same model, data, institution, ontology, or incentive.
- **Red-team capture or leakage:** the challenger becomes ceremonial, politically controlled, overprivileged, or a source of sensitive disclosure.
- **Metric gaming:** agents optimize proxy truth, social approval, or task scores without the intended property.
- **Dynamic-threshold discretion:** “soft like water” becomes an unlogged exception path.
- **Compute-right overreach:** arbitrary resource partition creates safety, scarcity, continuity, or bystander harms.
- **Anti-balkanization coercion:** latency or interoperability penalties make exit punitive or inaccessible.
- **Metaphysics laundering:** speculative ontology, theology, or consciousness claims become system authority.
- **Moral scalarization:** global consciousness/coherence optimization erases pluralism, distribution, consent, and floors.

## Threats And Governance Costs

Heterarchical verification adds real cost: distinct evaluators, independent ground truth, affected-party processes, calibration sets, access control, conflict disclosure, challenge handling, appeal, latency, maintenance, and cross-evaluator reconciliation. Resource-aware exit adds spare capacity, portability engineering, continuity funding, destination validation, and obligation transfer. These burdens must be measured alongside safety; otherwise the system can become unusable, inaccessible, or vulnerable to denial-of-service and strategic vetoes.
