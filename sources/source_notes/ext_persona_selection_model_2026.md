## Thesis

Considers assistant behavior as a persona elicited from pretraining character representations and refined in post-training.

## Mechanisms

Uses persona selection as an empirical/conceptual explanation and considers competing accounts of agency outside the visible Assistant persona, including lightweight persona routing.

## Evidence

Synthesizes behavioral and interpretability observations. Its authors leave exhaustiveness and future post-training effects open rather than identifying the complete source of every goal-directed action.

## Failure Modes

Persona-like behavior and controllable trait expression do not settle the location or completeness of learned agency. The framework is not a causal proof that no hidden objective or non-persona mechanism exists.
