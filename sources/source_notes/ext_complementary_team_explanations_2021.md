## Thesis

The mixed-method user study on three datasets measured how teams performed when explanations accompanied AI recommendations.

## Mechanisms

Explanations increased participants' acceptance of AI recommendations regardless of correctness. The studies did not demonstrate improved complementary performance from the explanations.

## Evidence

The book uses the selected source passage for the decision in “Count which recommendations a reviewer accepts”.
The worked example is illustrative.

## Failure Modes

These decision tasks have specific participants, models and interfaces. The result does not establish the effect of explanations on high-stakes approval or the quality of a real intervention path.
