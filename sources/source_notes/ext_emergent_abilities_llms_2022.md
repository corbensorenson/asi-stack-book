# Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models

Source ID: `ext_emergent_abilities_llms_2022`
Primary source: <https://openreview.net/forum?id=yzkSU5zdwD>

Wei et al. organize examples of task performance reported as absent in smaller
models and present in larger ones, motivating explicit study of sharpness and
predictability under scaling. The paper is used as a claim to test, not proof
of a universal phase transition or a mechanism.

Routing: `learning-theory-generalization-and-scaling-science`.
