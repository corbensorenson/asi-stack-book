# Source Note: When Combinations of Humans and AI Are Useful

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Source ID | `ext_human_ai_team_meta_analysis_2024` |
| Source title | When combinations of humans and AI are useful: A systematic review and meta-analysis |
| Authors / date | Michelle Vaccaro, Abdullah Almaatouq, and Thomas Malone; 2024 |
| Primary URL | https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-02024-1 |
| DOI | `10.1038/s41562-024-02024-1` |
| Evidence boundary | Preregistered synthesis of 106 experiments and 370 effect sizes within the included tasks, systems, and participant pools; not a universal law of human-AI teaming. |

## Thesis

The meta-analysis requires a three-arm comparison—human alone, AI alone, and
the combined system—and finds that combinations are not automatically better
than the stronger component. Average complementarity varies by task type and
by which component is stronger. This directly challenges a weak “centaur”
baseline that compares the team only with the human.

## Failure Modes

- The included experiments cover selected tasks and participant populations
  and may not represent longitudinal deployment.
- Aggregate effects can hide subgroup, expertise, learning, workload,
  dependence, and safety differences.
- Task performance does not measure mental integrity, skill retention,
  legitimacy, or beneficial neurotechnology.

## Book Chapters Supported

- `human-ai-symbiosis-neurotechnology-and-cognitive-sovereignty`: strong

- `human-ai-organizations-delegation-and-accountability`: team configuration

- `human-factors-and-meaningful-control-in-oversight`: operator performance is

## Mechanisms

- Require human-alone, AI-alone, and combination arms, then analyze
  complementarity relative to the strongest component and task moderators.

## Evidence

The preregistered meta-analysis synthesizes 106 experiments and 370 effect
sizes. The underlying tasks and populations bound every aggregate finding.

## Claims To Add Or Update

- Do not call a human-AI system synergistic merely because it beats the human;
  compare the combined system with both components.

## Open Questions

- Which longitudinal designs preserve skill, calibration, agency, and access
  while producing durable complementarity?
