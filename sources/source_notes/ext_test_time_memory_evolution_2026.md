## Thesis

Update external lessons and strategies rather than model weights during verifier-guided mathematical search.

## Mechanisms

TTME maintains operational guidelines and source-case-linked strategy families from evaluated candidates. Those memories condition later proposals while the model stays fixed; the discovery framework’s archive, parent selection and verifier are retained.

## Evidence

Reports comparisons on five mathematical construction tasks using two frameworks. TTT comparison results are taken from the original papers; not every arm is freshly rerun here. Reported runtime reductions exclude candidate evaluation, while memory-model calls are included.

## Failure Modes

Verifier validity is defined by successful compilation/scoring, not general scientific truth. Better construction scores and within-search reuse do not establish independent discovery, cross-task continual learning, universal superiority to weight updates or complete wall-time savings.
