## Thesis

Retain the vote distribution and dissenting rationales when recording a contested decision.

## Mechanisms

Compare distributions of human and model verdicts and categorize values expressed in their free-text rationales.

## Evidence

On 1,618 dilemmas, alignment declined as human disagreement rose and models expressed a narrower range of values.

## Failure Modes

Judgments reflect a particular online community. Representing a distribution supplies no authority to decide for that community.
