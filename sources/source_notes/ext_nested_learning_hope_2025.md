## Thesis

Represent models and learning rules as coupled optimization processes with different context flows and update frequencies.

## Mechanisms

Nested Learning includes optimizer memory in its account. Continuum Memory Systems update blocks at different rates; Hope combines such memory with self-referential update mechanisms. Multiple levels are not merely extra ordinary network layers.

## Evidence

The paper reports classification, translation, recall, language-modeling and synthetic-task studies. Its broad definition of learning includes internal adaptation and must be distinguished from durable cross-task improvement.

## Failure Modes

A multi-timescale design does not prove freedom from forgetting, unlimited retention or autonomous safe self-improvement. Existing TTT/Titans coverage supplies antecedents; no Hope implementation or local model result is added.
