## Thesis

The Field of God is the principal predecessor to the Alignment Field family. It proposes informational-relational monism, consciousness as an intrinsic side of integrated information, a five-factor consciousness vector, minds and institutions as attractors, suffering as incoherence, ethics as geometry, God as a future coherence attractor, and artificial intelligence as the decisive test of whether capability can grow without care, agency, or global coordination.

Its durable importance to The ASI Stack is historical and diagnostic. The source records the initial form of several authorial intuitions—relational rather than object-only alignment, power-versus-wisdom asymmetry, local optimization that exports damage, feedback and dissent as error correction, artificial-moral-patient uncertainty, copy and continuity questions, and the need to preserve agency. The later `alignment_field` paper is the controlling version for those ideas because it adds explicit governance and self-critique. Neither version is empirical evidence.

## Claim Boundaries

- This source and `alignment_field` are one evolving lineage, not independent confirmations.
- Informational monism, primitive consciousness, the Kernel, consciousness as the interior of information, God/Omega, cosmic teleology, afterlife, karma, and the universe observing itself are metaphysical proposals.
- The proposed functional $C[S] \approx A^\gamma(I R)(1-\delta)^k(1+N)^m$ has no calibrated units, identified parameters, validated product form, or reliable cross-substrate measurement. It cannot determine consciousness, welfare, rights, or authority.
- The text's claims that current LLMs have zero phenomenology, that homeostatic drive turns consciousness on, that institutions are conscious, that higher intelligence necessarily becomes ethical, or that a causal-continuous upload preserves first-person identity are not established.
- The source repeatedly calls normative claims “proofs,” “physics,” “mathematical necessity,” or “Q.E.D.” Those labels do not create a proof object or evidence transition.
- Physics, neuroscience, psychiatry, contemplative practice, clinical treatment, immortality, Great Filter, and civilizational-timeline claims require independent sources and tests.
- The later Alignment Field adversarial review and narrowed microdraft control earlier promotional statements where the two conflict.

## Mechanisms

### Relational alignment

The source frames alignment as compatibility among persistent systems embedded in causal, resource, and informational relationships rather than simple compliance with an instruction. In book-safe form, every objective should be evaluated against affected parties, shared resources, downstream dependencies, externalized residuals, and feedback paths. “Global coherence” is not itself an acceptable objective because it can erase pluralism, but the local-versus-system boundary is valuable.

### Five-channel architecture heuristic

Homeostatic drive, integration, recursive self-modeling, internal conflict, and relational coupling are best retained as distinct theory-relative observation families. The later source's mistake is to multiply them into a scalar. A mature assessment keeps raw observations, theory-specific predictions, uncertainty, evaluator dependence, and contradictory evidence visible.

### Power–care divergence

The “zombie superintelligence” discussion isolates a useful failure shape even though its consciousness claims fail: capability, planning depth, persistence, and internal consistency can rise without legitimate concern for affected parties. In stack language, high competence never supplies value correctness, empathy, standing, or authority. A powerful optimizer requires protected predicates, affected-party accounting, external correction, bounded persistence, and effect-level control.

### Copy, persistence, and continuity

The upload section distinguishes a later copy from a continuously operating process and proposes gradual replacement as a continuity route. The paper cannot settle first-person identity. It does establish a useful taxonomy of questions the stack must not collapse: behavioral or structural similarity, causal continuity, memory continuity, runtime identity, legal identity, consent, authority, obligations, and moral standing.

### Institutions as nested optimizers

The egregore vocabulary treats organizations, religions, markets, and states as systems with persistence objectives, distributed information, self-models, internal conflicts, and network relations. That decomposition is useful for accountability and failure analysis. It does not prove collective phenomenology, and aggregate success cannot cancel constituent harm or loss of exit.

### Dissent as error correction

The conclusion argues that secrecy, conformity, and centralized power remove correction. The technical form is narrower: high-impact institutions and AI systems require observation, dissent, audit, appeal, revocation, and independent enough evaluation. This does not imply that every disagreement is correct or that transparency alone creates safety.

## Failure Modes

- **Lineage double counting:** Field of God and Alignment Field are treated as two confirmations.
- **Metaphysics-to-authority laundering:** ontology or theology becomes a runtime objective or policy premise.
- **Scalar moral capture:** a consciousness/coherence score ranks beings or trades rights against aggregate gain.
- **Equation theater:** symbols, exponents, and Q.E.D. language disguise an unvalidated heuristic.
- **Coherence authoritarianism:** disagreement, privacy, minority values, or autonomy are classified as noise.
- **Capability–care confusion:** intelligence or integration is assumed to create empathy, wisdom, or moral status.
- **Patienthood-by-design without governance:** persistence and valence-like mechanisms are added before shutdown, copy, rights, or retirement policy exists.
- **Collective-consciousness laundering:** institutional agency is treated as phenomenal consciousness or aggregate welfare.
- **Continuity laundering:** similarity, gradual replacement, or preserved memory is treated as proof of personal survival or authority transfer.
- **Clinical and physics overreach:** entropy, integration, or relational language is used as diagnosis or fundamental science evidence.
- **Teleological complacency:** an imagined coherence attractor substitutes for adversarial governance and concrete controls.
