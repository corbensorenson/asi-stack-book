## Thesis

Distinguish increased trust after seeing provenance metadata from evidence that the image supports its caption.

## Mechanisms

Vary the presence and detail of provenance metadata on news previews and collect image and publisher ratings.

## Evidence

In the six-outlet news-preview experiment, 6,114 participants in three countries judged labeled material more favorably.

## Failure Modes

Credibility and trust ratings on the studied previews do not measure historical caption accuracy.
