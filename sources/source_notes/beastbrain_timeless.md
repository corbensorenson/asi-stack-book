## Thesis

### Claim boundary

The Timeless Edition removes the dated roadmap from BeastBrain v3.3.4 and
preserves one compact architecture snapshot: Mimic hardware adaptation,
HelixDB and neural memory, hybrid routed substrates, Portia navigation,
geometric and activation-based checking, browser perception, Digital SCIFs,
projected scaling, and illustrative pseudocode. “Timeless” describes its
editorial intent, not technical durability. Hardware APIs, model families,
security assumptions, references, and performance projections all age.

Because the main BeastBrain document contains the same edition, the two sources
must never be counted as separate support. The main family note controls the
version history and later corrections. This note records only the standalone
variant's exact section dispositions and any differences introduced by its
roadmap removal.

## Mechanisms

### Complete section audit

### Executive stack

The six-organ system usefully separates an operating vessel, hardware
adaptation, persistent memory, planning/routing, verification, and perception.
The ASI Stack retains the separation while discarding claims of autopoiesis,
hardware agnosticism, mathematically verified thought, and infinite context.
The vertical organism diagram is not an architecture proof and omits
cross-cutting authority, evidence, state, failure, and recovery flows.

### Mimic hardware handshake and modes

Thermal probing, memory-topology discovery, device-specific execution modes,
pressure response, and battery-aware background throttling motivate a
hardware-profile decision. The ASI Stack generalizes beyond four named device
classes and requires versioned measurements, staged qualification, sustained
workload evidence, conservative fallback, expiry, and safe transitions.

The claimed direct SSD-to-accelerator paths, zero-copy behavior, Apple Neural
Engine access, 500 ms eviction rule, realtime priority, and sub-five-second
adaptation are source proposals. “Panic paging” at 99% pressure is specifically
not retained as policy: emergency mutation can destroy the state, quality, or
rollback path it is supposed to save.

### HelixDB and neural long-term memory

Graph and vector stores, persistent artifact state, surprise-prioritized
consolidation, and test-time memory are useful categories. The displayed
surprise-minus-variance formula does not establish aleatoric/epistemic
separation, useful novelty, poison resistance, or a safe update threshold.
Every weight change requires source rights, a complete mutable-state manifest,
isolation, qualification, and rollback.

The consolidation pseudocode is not executable as written and conflates the
gradient target, input, loss, and persistent optimizer state. The manual
`evolve_self_trigger` deliberately multiplies recent loss weights to force
updates and is retained only as a negative example: a URI, user action, or
high-surprise label cannot bypass update governance.

### Hybrid flows and intelligence tiers

Routing routine, procedural, and novel tasks to different kernels is durable.
The exact Grassmann/DyDiLA equations, entropy thresholds, scaling labels, and
geometric-verification claims are not. Orthogonality or geodesic adherence is
a mathematical property of a representation, not proof that a generated
statement is true. Entropy is not a general proxy for task difficulty, risk,
or required reasoning. The book's Routing Heads and Replaceable Substrates
chapters own the stronger interface and comparison program.

### Portia navigation

Coordinate learning, relation prediction, multi-hop training, lookahead,
distractor filtering, and residual stabilization are already superseded by the
complete TreeLLM–Spider–Portia lineage audit. The fixed hash width, edge count,
three-hop search, KL threshold, and hundred-hop language are proposals, not
results. This variant supplies no extra evidentiary weight.

### Aletheia and the Geometer

Invariant tracking and an activation-space sycophancy detector can be scoped
verification signals. Neither deterministically detects hallucination or
deference. A valid geometric path can encode a false proposition; an invalid
path can still produce a true one; activation directions can drift, correlate
with benign behavior, and be gamed. The pseudocode checks adjacent subspace
distance and determinant but never establishes the advertised semantic
refinement.

### Sensory cortex and `brain://`

Joint DOM and rendered-image inspection is a sound multimodal pressure because
either view can omit or contradict the other. Cached perception must retain
source, observation time, viewport, transformations, uncertainty, and expiry.
The proposed URI operations are request names only. Ingest, recall, connection,
and especially self-evolution require separate rights and authority.

### Digital SCIF and side channels

Ephemeral compartments, late credential injection, explicit zeroization, and
cache-side-channel awareness are useful threat-model components. A software
context window is not an enclave; `zeroize` does not remove secrets from every
copy, register, accelerator, page cache, log, backup, socket, or external
recipient; and cache partitioning alone does not close speculative or timing
channels. The dedicated Ladon/Manhattan and Context Engineer sources control
the more complete lineage.

### Projections, validation plan, and appendices

The eight projected advantages—linear or infinite memory, zero false
positives, 90% power reduction, continual gains, sub-five-second adaptation,
near-zero leaks, constant verification overhead, and sub-50 ms distributed
scaling—remain unsupported. Naming three hardware classes is not an experiment
design. A valid study needs artifacts, workloads, baselines, complete costs,
quality and safety metrics, faults, multi-seed runs, independent evaluators,
and retained failures.

The pseudocode is useful only as algorithm-intent documentation. Several
functions contain placeholders, shape ambiguities, undefined globals, or
unsafe update semantics. The reference list is a discovery queue; claims from
those works are not inherited without primary-source review.

## Failure Modes

- Counting the standalone export and its embedded main-document copy twice.
- Treating “Timeless” or “Diamond Master” as evidence of currentness or
  maturity.
- Converting theoretical component properties into semantic truth, safety, or
  whole-system performance.
- Allowing a self-evolution URI or forced loss amplification to bypass training
  authority and qualification.
- Treating hardware discovery, memory mapping, zeroization, cache partitioning,
  or content fit as an end-to-end guarantee.
- Using pseudocode shape, citations, or a three-device validation list as an
  implementation or experiment.

## Manuscript decision

Its two useful deltas—evergreen framing and the consolidated Mimic description—
are already represented by the main family audit and the new hardware-profile
qualification section. Treating both documents as evidence would inflate one
authored design into two observations.
