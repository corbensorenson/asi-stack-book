## Thesis

WHO separates neuroimaging, brain-computer interfaces, neuromodulation, and
neurological devices and notes both rapid technical development and limited,
challenging adoption in health settings. That distinction helps the book keep
measurement, decoding, actuation, therapy, assistance, and enhancement from
collapsing into one “neural interface” category.

## Failure Modes

- Landscape coverage does not validate a device, model, treatment, or
  regulatory regime.
- Health use does not authorize employment, education, military, consumer, or
  coercive use.
- Neural signal access and neural actuation have different risk envelopes.

## Mechanisms

- Separate neural sensing, decoding, assistive control, closed-loop adaptation,
  and stimulation while tracking health, access, equity, rights, and governance.

## Evidence

The WHO landscape analysis synthesizes global-health opportunities and
challenges. It is not evidence of individual device efficacy or safety.

## Claims To Add Or Update

- Treat neural and inferred mental data as high-sensitivity, purpose-bound
  records and scale evidence with the causal power of the intervention.

## Open Questions

- How can benefits, evidence, governance capacity, and remedy extend beyond
  wealthy health systems and dominant-language populations?
