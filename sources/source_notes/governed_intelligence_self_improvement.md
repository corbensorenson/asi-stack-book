## Thesis

Self-improvement becomes reflexive when a candidate changes the machinery that generates, evaluates, authorizes, deploys, or learns from future changes. A candidate must not inherit the authority or evidence of the component it replaces, and model-shaped future evidence must not automatically serve as independent validation of the lineage that produced it.

## Mechanisms

The paper joins versioned capability replacement, full-state migration, property-specific evidence inheritance, recursive-depth control, reflexive model-world dynamics, evidence ancestry, grounding reserves, descendant noninheritance, and improvement-velocity limits.

## Failure Modes

- A candidate writes or selects the tests that ratify its own replacement.
- State migration preserves schema while losing semantics, recovery, or descendant obligations.
- A low-traffic canary performs one unbounded irreversible action.
- Descendants inherit credentials, qualification, or reproduction authority through lineage rather than fresh admission.
