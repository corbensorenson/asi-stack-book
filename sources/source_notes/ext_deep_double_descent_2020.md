# Deep Double Descent

Source ID: `ext_deep_double_descent_2020`
Primary source: <https://openreview.net/forum?id=-bJ_Hb7lCVH>

Nakkiran et al. report model-wise, sample-wise, and epoch-wise double-descent
behavior and propose effective model complexity as a unifying lens. The chapter
uses this as a failure of one-dimensional “more capacity/data is always better”
forecasting. The reported regimes are empirical and configuration-bound; they
do not establish a universal law or predict a particular frontier run.

Routing: `learning-theory-generalization-and-scaling-science`.
