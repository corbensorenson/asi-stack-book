# A Causal Calculus for Statistical Research

Source ID: `ext_causal_calculus_1995`
Source: <https://proceedings.mlr.press/r0/pearl95a.html>

Pearl gives formal semantics and rules for identifying interventional
quantities from observational distributions under a structural causal model.
The calculus separates seeing from doing. It depends on the causal graph and
assumptions; it does not discover the true graph from arbitrary data, validate
an ontology, or make counterfactuals reliable by syntax alone.

Routing: Governed World Models and Reality Grounding.
