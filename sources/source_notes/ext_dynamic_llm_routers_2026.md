# Dynamic LLM Routers are Often Misguided

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Source ID | `ext_dynamic_llm_routers_2026` |

## Thesis

Compare learned assignment with random assignment between a fixed, well-chosen eligible pair at matched spend.

## Mechanisms

Compare a query-dependent router with random assignment to two chosen models at matched realized cost.

## Evidence

None of six commercial routers across 14 settings beat the matched-cost two-model random baseline on the benchmark.

## Failure Modes

The comparisons concern whole-model inference services with particular rosters, prices and tasks.
