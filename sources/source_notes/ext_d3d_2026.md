# D3D: Decoupled Diffusion Drafting for Asynchronous Speculative Decoding

Baoyou Chen et al. (2026). [Primary publication](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06425v1).

## Mechanism and evidence

Use a prefix-conditioned drafter to prepare successor blocks while the target verifies the current block. Reuse the successor matching the accepted path.

For MATH-500 with D3D-1.7B and Qwen3-32B, aggregate decoding throughput rose from 154 to 864 tokens/s between one and sixteen requests. Speedup over ordinary decoding fell from 6.1× to 2.7×.

## Review scope and open question

Sections 3–4, especially Table 2. Table 2 measures aggregate decode throughput on the authors’ backend. It leaves the per-request latency distribution, queueing and other workloads open.
