# Proof-of-Learning: Definitions and Practice

Source ID: `ext_proof_of_learning_2021`
Source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.05633>

Jia et al. propose checkpoint and stochastic-training evidence to show that
final parameters resulted from a claimed iterative learning process. The book
uses it as a training-provenance comparator and pairs it with the known need
for adversarial security review. A proof of computation or learning history
does not prove lawful data, objective legitimacy, absence of poisoning,
correct evaluation, useful capability, or safety.
