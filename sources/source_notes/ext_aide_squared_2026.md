## Thesis

Keep inherited search history explicit when attributing a successor gain.

## Mechanisms

Accepted rewrites of a research agent change how its successor searches and manages context.

## Evidence

The authors report seven improvements over eight days and transfer to four held-out benchmarks.

## Failure Modes

A bounded lineage and finite benchmark suite leave the long-run rate of improvement open.
