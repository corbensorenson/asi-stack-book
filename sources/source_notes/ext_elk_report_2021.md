# Eliciting Latent Knowledge

Source ID: `ext_elk_report_2021`
Source: <https://www.alignment.org/blog/arcs-first-technical-report-eliciting-latent-knowledge/>

ARC frames an open problem: how to map from a model's internal world model to
human-understandable answers when ordinary training may reward a convincing
report rather than a true one. The book uses ELK as a target for white-box
evidence and ontology translation, not as a solved method. Probes, reporters,
and consistency tests can all share the same blind spot.

Routing: `white-box-evidence-interpretability-and-activation-governance`.
