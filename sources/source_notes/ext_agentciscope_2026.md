## Thesis

Check each protected attribute against its destination separately from completing the requested task.

## Mechanisms

Build executable multi-step workspace tasks with protected attributes and inspect tool actions for prohibited disclosure.

## Evidence

Across 12 agents, single-run leakage affected 32.5% to 94.4% of the 936 benchmark tasks.

## Failure Modes

The tasks use synthesized, explicit disclosure constraints and single-agent environments.
