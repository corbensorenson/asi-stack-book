## Thesis

CoilMoECOT proposes a prime-temporal trace feature family as one optional specialist lane inside a governed MoECOT runtime. Its strongest book contribution is a placement-and-promotion contract: add the coil first where traces are native, keep it removable, train diagnostics from clean ledgers, begin in shadow mode, and promote only when a matched benchmark distinguishes it from simpler rules and classifiers.

## Mechanisms

- Prime temporal sets, trace edges, and antinodes encode candidate lag and recurrence relationships.
- The recommended adoption order is Graph/Trace first, Concept second, Token last, because earlier layers expose clearer events and failure attribution.
- Clean run ledgers generate next-action, future-mask, risk, and anomaly examples without granting the coil policy authority.
- Insertion points are explicit: a pre-planner risk prior, post-plan risk scan, and post-run distillation lane.
- Anti-experts emit bounded penalties or inhibition evidence, not negative mixture weights or silent suppression.
- The coil starts in shadow mode and remains removable; benchmarks, canaries, rollback, and declared update slices govern promotion.
- Use is conditional on sufficient trace data, trace-like tasks, and measurable advantage. A simple rule or classifier remains the preferred baseline when adequate.

## Failure Modes

- Making the coil substrate load-bearing before a removable shadow comparison.
- Applying recurrence at token level first, where attribution and compute costs are hardest to isolate.
- Treating an anti-expert as an authority to suppress otherwise correct behavior.
- Training on contaminated or incomplete traces and then interpreting its outputs as causal risk evidence.
- Allowing weight updates outside declared slices or promotion without rollback.
- Counting repeated appended MoECOT material as multiple supporting sources.

## Claims To Add Or Update

- Treat negative-signal specialists as bounded evidence lanes whose penalties, affected candidates, confidence, and override policy remain visible.
- Prefer the earliest trace-native insertion point that can be isolated and removed.
- Require a no-coil path, simple baseline, placement ablation, multiseed test, canary, and rollback before promotion.

## Open Questions

- Do prime-lag features beat ordinary temporal features, graph statistics, or small classifiers on any preregistered trace workload?
- Which insertion point yields separable benefit after latency, memory, governance, and false-inhibition costs?
- Can anti-expert penalties be calibrated without teaching a hidden veto channel?
