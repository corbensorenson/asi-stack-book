# Examining the Impact of Provenance-Enabled Media on Trust and Accuracy Perceptions

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Publication | K. J. Kevin Feng et al. (2023). [Primary publication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12118v3). |

## Thesis

Test how people interpret incomplete provenance on an honest image.

## Mechanisms

Vary provenance completeness and validity when participants judge media shared on social platforms.

## Evidence

In an experiment with 595 US and UK participants, incomplete or invalid provenance sometimes reduced belief in honest media.

## Failure Modes

The experiment measured reactions to particular media and provenance displays.
