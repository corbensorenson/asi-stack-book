# Legal Alignment for Safe and Ethical AI

Source ID: `ext_legal_alignment_2026`
Source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04175>

The paper develops a research agenda around compliance with legitimate legal
rules, legal interpretation methods, and institutional concepts for AI
reliability and cooperation. Law is jurisdictional, changing, contested, and
sometimes unjust or internally conflicting. Legal alignment is therefore a
governed input and process, not a synonym for moral alignment or an automatic
answer produced by a language model.
