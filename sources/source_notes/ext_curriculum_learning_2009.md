# Curriculum Learning

Source ID: `ext_curriculum_learning_2009`
Source: <https://doi.org/10.1145/1553374.1553380>

Bengio et al. propose ordering training material from easier concepts or
examples toward harder ones. The book uses curriculum as a versioned training
policy with explicit difficulty, ordering, promotion, and coverage. A
curriculum can improve optimization in some settings or create blind spots,
bias, forgotten hard cases, and safety regressions in others.
