## Thesis

Transfer the procedure and requalify its output on the destination.

## Mechanisms

An executable transformation can use each destination network and its local examples to derive a fresh parameter change.

## Evidence

The copying and rerunning comparison spans ten programs and five independently trained vision transformers. Recomputed changes transferred substantially better than copied deltas.

## Failure Modes

The destination models shared the architecture. Clean-image accuracy measures only one retention condition.
