# When does verification capacity change the admission decision?

Predict parity on admitted work at every rate under one owner, ordered checks and durable task state. Predict a backlog once offered work exceeds the four checks available per tick. An explicit obligation record could earn its extra writes if it completes more supported jobs, reduces wait or avoids an unsupported acceptance. On parity prefer the ordinary queue. This tests the representation of pending verification work under a shared scheduling rule.

Send the same 32 jobs (eight repeats of one-, three-, two- and two-check tasks) at 1, 2 or 4 jobs per logical tick. Inspect through the end of tick 15, including arrivals at tick zero. At low load, jobs arriving after the cutoff are outside the observed denominator. Use a second equal-cohort measurement at the end of each lane's last arrival tick and a final fully drained check. Checks are FIFO in task and then obligation order. Each fourth task fails its last check. Both queues have the same evidence, may withhold acceptance, and have four checks per tick. The ordinary queue persists one progress cursor per task. The explicit variant materializes each pending requirement and records its result. Measure application record writes by counting insertions and updates to task or obligation state, excluding common ingress, scheduling and output. State the unit and cost boundary.

Use an independent oracle over the required checks to score acceptance. Record unsupported acceptances, unfinished individual checks (including untouched tasks already arrived), completed verified jobs, rejected jobs, and average arrival-to-verdict ticks for completed jobs. A same-tick verdict waits zero ticks. Report the age of pending jobs so completed-only wait cannot hide backlog. Retain job-level outcomes for all three observations. Require equal cohorts at drain, conservation of arrived checks, four or fewer executions per tick and detection of a deliberately premature acceptance in scorer calibration. One deterministic workload supplies mechanism evidence only. Human time and production capacity are outside this comparison.
