4. **The bound is on modeled total cost.** Given that the finite selector returns a route and the alternative is eligible and belongs to the same candidate list, the selected route's modeled total cost is at most the alternative's modeled total cost. The same conditional guarantee applies to an external workload represented by such a list, eligibility predicate, and cost model. Applying it to an arbitrary external workload requires establishing those premises and validating the model against measured costs before claiming production efficiency.
