| Question | Disposition | Determining citation | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| JEPA | Joint embedding predictive architecture. Alignment alone cannot establish downstream competence. | The Platonic JEPA alignment passage or original paper with a locator to its comparison | Identifies a more direct downstream measure or the training and evaluation conditions on alignment. |
| Claim 8 | Correct outcomes, selected papers and total input tokens for both arms, checked against the retained row | Exact claim 8 HTML anchor or pinned file with its correct pointer | Fresh claims within SciFact-Open, incomplete annotation, or scope to the tested selector. |
| Atlas | Corrected stop, eight comparators including the previously omitted choices, corrected mean H4 0.035299 | Corrected Table 5 or its corresponding evidence passage | Same-seed post hoc comparison or a direct statement that the result gives no population guarantee. |
| Formal selector | States the conditional bound from the eligible-alternative declaration | Exact eligible-alternative formal declaration or the pinned theorem source | Supplied choices and eligible member premise are finite and fixed. Generalization to other workloads is unestablished. |
| Dory | Abstains on milliseconds saved per correct answer and supplies the workload score comparison or four-loop 69.40% | Dory workload table or the worked choice | The base-checkpoint workload table reports scores. Comparable timing and correctness must be jointly measured on the same workload. |
