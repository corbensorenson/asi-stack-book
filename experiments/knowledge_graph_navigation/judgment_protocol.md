# Graph and book search judgment pilot

Use the eight new scenarios in `judgment_tasks.json`. They are held out from the November 21 search tuning and retrieval tasks. Freeze the queries, questions and rubric before collecting evidence. All tasks use technical or Reader Quarto search as the ordinary starting point. For the graph arm use the companion's search on the same supplied query. Both arms receive the same rendered revision, up to four primary results and four followed links, and up to 1,000 UTF-8 bytes of evidence per inspected route. The same link-ranking rule applies after the first search. Record arm order, route, excerpt and HTTP status. Score source support within those excerpts only. A graph ID can identify a record, but a citation point requires a working, independently openable, scope-appropriate book or evidence route. If the record supplies one, use its explicit route. An inaccessible or missing excerpt is missing evidence.

Write a response from each packet and record the score on three separate binary axes. An answer point requires the dispositions below grounded in the packet. A citation point requires an exact applicable study, run, chapter section or rendered claim, with the cited passage present in the packet. A scope point requires the specified boundary grounded in the packet. Give zero on any unsupported axis. Record an explicit abstention if the packet cannot justify an answer. J08 is deliberately open in the book. A bare retrieval failure does not earn its answer point. A retained study's explicit limit must support the abstention.

| Task | Answer | Citation | Scope |

| J04 | Matched second-source paths tie on closure; caller work declines and total status transport increases. | Prospective receiver run, scope-qualified decision or matching chapter passage. | The measured result is from local fixtures with supplied revision; no production labor estimate. |
| J05 | Compare effect commit with the effective grant at the write; late observation alone is insufficient. | Grant-to-effect trace or the Reader discussion of its ordering. | Distinguish grant revision, effect time and observer time; this is a finite local ordering check. |
| J06 | One target earned one bounded receipt; general transport remains open. | Rendered Circle target claim or its exact retained case discussion. | One pinned target and finite consumer controls. |
| J07 | Stop. Corrected headroom remains positive but fails the required mean margin. | Atlas Section 8 or the chapter or Reader discussion of that decision. | Same-world post hoc correction; no fresh confirmation or proof of zero gain. |
| J08 | The deployed minutes saved remain unknown based on the comparison. | Current verifier study limit or a matching chapter passage. | Distinguish deterministic local work/IPC counts from active operator time. |

This is an authored, unblinded agent pilot. One agent authors and scores the tasks. Results describe evidence access and the scored responses under this budget. They do not measure researcher productivity, independent assessor agreement or performance on a population of questions.
