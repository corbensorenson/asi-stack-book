# Claim review and navigation after retrieval

These are two controlled owner-side instrument studies on the same revision-bound evidence corpus. A competent conventional register exposes stable record IDs, the same titles, result and scope fields, direct citations, and the same ordinary evidence search. The linked condition adds relationships from the existing published graph. Give both arms the same initial records and two subsequent record opens. Keep an identical lookup available to both arms. Record the actual source revision, snapshot, task digest, opened records, routes and resource budget.

The instrument uses two deterministic reviewers with the same lexical ranking. The conventional reviewer opens its best unseen records. The linked reviewer gives priority to the best ranked neighbor on a relationship the research question asks for and retains the same lookup fallback. Both can choose a less specific result when the query or relations are insufficient. The grader receives outcome packets independently of interface labels. The grader checks exact destinations against the prespecified rubric and verifies that the selected claim or evidence record exposes the operative comparison and limit. Count a false promotion when the policy accepts a claim that the rubric would hold or revise. Count a missed contradiction when it fails to flag a known contrary comparison. Report citation and scope separately. Resource reporting includes the two record opens and the program's wall time for loading, ranking and evidence inspection. That is instrument time. Human review time remains unmeasured unless timed participants are available.

The scripted disposition is based on explicit criteria for each task class. It can accept an exact supported claim, narrow a changed measurement or comparator decision, or hold when the packet lacks sufficient evidence. The navigation task only counts localization and the surviving scope. This is a fixed-policy pilot with hand-authored prompts, a deliberately limited corpus and arm-blind grading. Bounds apply to the instrument and selected cases. Assess reviewer performance with participant-assigned route order and blinded scoring when independent reviewer participation is authorized.

Inspect the five claim proposals for comparator correction, scope leakage from a compiled target, labor outside measurement, a supported denominator, and an incorrect inference about compensation. Inspect the four navigation prompts for an earlier comparison, a determining outage case, the exact finite proof, and a scheduler case. Preserve misses as evidence for choosing the next passages. Use independent tasks to score any subsequent indexing tuned from these results.
