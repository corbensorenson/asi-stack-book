# Formal declarations

**PD1.** For an accepted secret-injection event, what stages does the Lean proposition require before and after the event? Name the modeled actor, the three Boolean conditions and the time inequality. Can acceptance establish coverage of a deployed sensor?

**PD2.** The finite route selector returns a route. An eligible, listed alternative has the same modeled cost. Does the minimum-cost proposition rule out that tie, force a unique winner, or say how the ties are broken? Give its inequality and the hypotheses on both the selection and the comparison route.

**PD3.** Compare the participation-signals and commitment-signals declarations. What type of signals does each classifier receive, what predicate would it have to match, and what universal guarantee does each declaration reject? What would a reviewer still need to establish about actual institutional outcomes?

**PD4.** A Circle fixture reports all three promotion flags false but leaves the unsupported-transfer rejection flag false. Which of the two indexed guard propositions settles this specific case, and why does the other proposition's antecedent fail to settle it? Can those fixture flags establish that the mutation trials actually ran?
