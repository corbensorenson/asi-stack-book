# Applying formal results

**PA1.** A provider restores service while a harmed group still needs a remedy. When does the societal-resilience chapter allow closing the incident, and what outcome must remain distinct from service restoration? Use the provider-signals declaration to explain why the provider's own status cannot establish population recovery.

**PA2.** Suppose the modeled secret-injection event was accepted and a release note later claims that a controller detected oscillation safely. What can reviewing the stop event and available sensor trace establish? What evidence would support the causal claim, and what should a reviewer do if the decisive instrument was missing? Cite the Lean boundary and the book's application guidance.

**PA3.** A Circle consumer has a finite theorem and matching fingerprint. Its replay state and expiry are unresolved. Can the consumer use the receipt yet, and what additional justification would it need before claiming better model behavior? Cite both an exact Circle consumer-gate declaration and the chapter's rule.

**PA4.** A proposed route is cheaper under the model but fails the quality check. Is it a counterexample to the finite selector's guarantee? Give the relevant condition in Lean, name the other cheaper countermodel described in the Reader, and say what evidence would still be needed to claim a production saving.
