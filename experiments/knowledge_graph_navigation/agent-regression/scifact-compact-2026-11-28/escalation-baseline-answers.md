## SE1

The study used the official labeled SciFact train split to develop and freeze thresholds, then reserved the official labeled dev split for evaluation. It excluded dev claim 3, the dataset-card example, and every claim that shared its cited paper. It also removed training claims that shared any citation with the remaining reserved dev claims. Annotations were used to partition and score, and the official unlabeled test was outside the study. These exclusions control overlap within the local study. The reserved claims are still from a public split, and the frozen model’s pretraining exposure is unknown. [Pinned SciFact release protocol, “Freeze and adjudicate”](https://github.com/corbensorenson/asi-stack-book/blob/6e782c3b914451609f9f73bd063071e70bb440dc/experiments/scifact_citation_check/protocol.md#freeze-and-adjudicate)

## SE2

For each of the first three documents, the selector summed the three-class vectors from the context reading and the exact-citation reading. It maximized `max(summed SUPPORT, summed CONTRADICT) − summed NEUTRAL`, breaking ties in favor of the earlier document in the original TF–IDF ranking. The selected document’s highest-scoring label determined the verdict, with neutral held. Annotation labels were consulted after selection to score the outcome. The protocol tested this selector on 213 previously unscored claims from the same SciFact-Open benchmark. [Pinned three-paper selection protocol](https://github.com/corbensorenson/asi-stack-book/blob/c965253fceea696df439dbd60bf8fbe54220d725/experiments/scifact_three_papers/protocol.md)

## SE3

For claim 83, the annotated paper was *Characterization of the adipocyte cellular lineage in vivo* (18064113). Its citation-provenance annotation was SUPPORT with zero-based highlight indices `[4, 5]`, so the two-sentence limit allowed a certifiable citation. The TF–IDF baseline chose *Differential sympathetic outflow to adipose depots is required for visceral fat loss in response to calorie restriction* (54468511). The BM25 document-ranker change chose *Biglycan Deletion Alters Adiponectin Expression in Murine Adipose Tissue and 3T3-L1 Adipocytes* (15432317). The sentence-only and combined changes returned the same papers as their respective document rankers. The returned papers were unjudged. All four arms predicted NEUTRAL and held, so none released a verdict or returned the annotated paper despite its presence in the candidate catalog. [Paired SciFact-Open case 83](https://corbensorenson.github.io/asi-stack-book/products/evidence-registry/study-citation-retrieval.html#run-fresh-citation-retrieval--case-83)

## SE4

From the repository root, run `python experiments/scifact_citation_check/score.py select` to select and save the frozen policies using only development predictions. Then run `python experiments/scifact_citation_check/score.py evaluate` to verify the saved policies against development and score the reserved predictions. This checks the decisions conditional on the retained identifiers, probability vectors, citation matches, and cost counters. It leaves reproduction of those inputs by fresh retrieval, citation selection, and model inference untested. [Pinned SciFact release protocol, “Reproduce”](https://github.com/corbensorenson/asi-stack-book/blob/6e782c3b914451609f9f73bd063071e70bb440dc/experiments/scifact_citation_check/protocol.md#reproduce)
