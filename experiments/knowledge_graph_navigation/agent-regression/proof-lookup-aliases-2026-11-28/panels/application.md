# Proof and application confirmation

**PC1.** A reviewer has a matching Circle receipt fingerprint and an earlier replay, but a fresh report marks the stale-contract rejection control as false. The publisher wants to reuse the receipt for a new deployment claim. What does the finite consumer gate require now, and what review would still be needed to support the deployment claim? Cite the deciding Lean guard and the chapter’s rule for using a receipt.

**PC2.** A finite selector returns the least expensive eligible route under declared costs. Its review still has an open obligation with no recorded residual. Can the minimum-cost result justify promoting the selection to a measured efficiency claim? Give the exact comparison that Lean establishes, how the Reader treats the open obligation, and which judgments still require empirical evidence.

**PC3.** A regulator signed an agreement, published duties and named a verifier. The reviewer has no observation of whether people could actually reach a remedy. What does the formal commitment-signals result imply about an inference of effective enforcement? Would it imply that this particular agreement was ineffective? Cite the declaration and the Reader’s boundary for judging real outcomes.

**PC4.** A provider reports restored service and direct remedies for known recipients. It has not verified public correction or adaptation. Which outcome categories must the research record keep distinct, and why does the provider-signals theorem leave those unobserved outcomes unresolved? Cite the formal result and the operational rule.
