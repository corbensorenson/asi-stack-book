Freeze the cohort and selector before generating predictions. The 65-claim SciFact-Open rank audit motivated k=3. Use the other SciFact-Open claims with previously unscored claim IDs, excluding public examples 170 and 263 and any source or cited paper shared with those 65 claims. Include every eligible claim (213). Earlier studies may have retrieved or indexed the same papers. This is a fresh claim comparison within a familiar benchmark and model. Model pretraining exposure is unknown.

Use the digest-pinned archive, quantized MiniLM, tokenizer and environment from the preceding retrieval study. Both arms use the complete released candidate catalog of nonempty abstracts, the same title-doubled unigram/bigram TF–IDF ranker and stable input-order ties.
